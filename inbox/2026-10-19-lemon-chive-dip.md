# Lemon Chive Dip

source: ChatGPT + Anna  
added_by: anna  
via: chatgpt  
serves: snack-sized batch  
time: 5 minutes

**Ingredients**
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1–2 teaspoons mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon Dijon mustard
- Fresh lemon juice, to taste (amount not given)
- Fresh chives, chopped, to taste (amount not given)
- Freshly ground black pepper, to taste (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Add the sour cream, mayonnaise, and Dijon mustard to a small bowl and stir until smooth.
2. Add a squeeze of fresh lemon juice and mix well.
3. Stir in chopped fresh chives and freshly ground black pepper to taste.
4. Taste and adjust with additional lemon, chives, or black pepper as desired.
5. Serve immediately, or refrigerate until ready to serve.

**Notes**
Originally made to pair with Trader Joe’s Irish Garlic Potato Chips. The cool, tangy lemon-and-chive dip balances the rich, buttery garlic flavor of the chips especially well.
