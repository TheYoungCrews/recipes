A big-batch Rosita — reposado tequila, Campari, and both vermouths — clarified with a milk wash until it's silky and smooth, then bottled to keep on hand for friends — from Justin Sajda (@thirstywhale_).

## Original

Batch:
- 16 oz Campari
- 16 oz dry vermouth
- 16 oz sweet vermouth
- 48 oz reposado tequila
- 20-ish dashes Angostura bitters ("20sh" in the caption)
- Pinch of salt (seen in the video)

Milk wash:
- About 1 1/2 cups whole milk

Serve:
- Large ice cube, coupe glass
- Expressed lemon peel

Steps (batch spec from the caption; wash and service from the video):
1. Combine the Campari, dry vermouth, sweet vermouth, reposado, Angostura, and a pinch of salt in a large pitcher and stir.
2. Add the whole milk — the mixture curdles. Cover and let it sit (he says the whole wash is done within about 3 hours).
3. Strain slowly through a cheesecloth-lined strainer into a clean vessel, letting the curds themselves act as the filter. Bottle the clarified cocktail.
4. To serve, pour over a large ice cube in a coupe and express a lemon peel over the top.

He describes the result as oily, silky, and viscous, and not overly bitter.

## Notes

Added 2026-11-12 from an Instagram reel by @thirstywhale_ (Justin Sajda). Spirit spec from the caption; the milk amount (about 1 1/2 cups), pinch of salt, straining method, and service are from the video. Serves (32, at a 3 oz pour from the 96 oz batch) is my estimate.
