@foodie.randy's red curry shortrib ramen — steak-style seared short rib over noodles in a quick red curry coconut broth (Easy Meals for 2, ep. 46).

## Original

Ingredients:
- 400-500 g short rib
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 cloves garlic (for the short rib), plus 2-3 cloves garlic (for the broth)
- 3 tbsp red curry paste
- 1 tsp ginger
- 1 can coconut milk
- 800 ml water
- 1 tsp chicken bouillon
- 1 tbsp sugar
- juice of half a lime, to taste
- a few drops fish sauce, to taste
- 200 g noodles (creator used 3-minute instant noodles)
- bok choy or spinach (optional)
- egg (optional)
- chili oil, for garnish
- green onions, for garnish
- salt and black pepper

Steps:
1. Season the short rib with salt and pepper on all sides and pat dry.
2. Sear it like a steak, basting in the butter, garlic, and soy sauce, until it reaches your desired doneness. Remove the short rib and garlic from the pan, leaving about half the garlic butter behind.
3. In the same pan over medium heat, add the red curry paste, garlic, and ginger; let the garlic develop flavor, then mix.
4. Pour in the coconut milk and water, mix well with the curry paste, and bring to a gentle simmer.
5. Season with the chicken bouillon, fish sauce, sugar, and lime juice, tasting as you go — the curry paste is already salty, so don't add bouillon per its package directions.
6. Add the noodles and simmer until cooked. If using starchy noodles (ramen or rice noodles), cook them in a separate pot and finish them in the broth so the broth doesn't turn slimy. Add the bok choy or spinach now.
7. Optional jammy egg: boil 6 minutes 30 seconds, then straight into an ice bath.
8. Serve the sliced short rib over the noodles and broth, garnished with chili oil and green onions (and the egg, if made).

## Our version

Use liquid aminos in place of the soy sauce. Otherwise same as original.
