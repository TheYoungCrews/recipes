Cosy chilli-spiked broth over rice with sticky caramelised soy chicken and crisp greens. Creator: @thegoodbite.

## Original
> Best-guess reconstruction: the creator keeps the exact recipe in their app, so this is inferred from the video. All amounts below are estimates.

**Ingredients**
- 1.5 lb boneless chicken thighs
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp grated ginger
- 2 cups white rice
- 4 cups chicken stock
- 2 tbsp chilli crisp, plus more to finish
- 1 bunch broccolini
- 1 cup snap peas
- 1 tbsp sesame seeds, black and white
- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 1 lime

**Steps**
1. Marinate the chicken in the soy sauce, honey, and grated ginger for at least 20 minutes.
2. Cook the white rice and keep it covered.
3. Sear the chicken hard in the avocado oil until deeply caramelised and sticky at the edges. Rest, then slice.
4. In the same pan, soften sliced garlic and ginger in the rendered fat, pour in the chicken stock, stir in the chilli crisp, and simmer a few minutes.
5. Blanch the broccolini and snap peas in the broth for the last 2-3 minutes until bright and crisp.
6. Invert a packed bowl of rice into the center of each wide bowl, ladle the broth around it, fan the sliced chicken alongside, tuck in the greens, and finish with chilli crisp, sesame seeds, and lime.
