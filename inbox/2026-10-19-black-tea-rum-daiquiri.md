# Black Tea Rum Daiquiri

**Yield:** 1 cocktail
**Profile:** Tropical, rich, crisp, subtly malty

### Ingredients
- 2 oz El Dorado rum
- ¾ oz fresh lime juice
- ½ oz homemade English breakfast tea simple syrup
- 1 dash aromatic bitters (optional)
- Lime wheel or twist for garnish (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Combine rum, lime juice, tea syrup, and optional bitters in a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill with ice and shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.
3. Double-strain into a chilled coupe.
4. Garnish with a lime wheel or twist.

### Recipe Notes
- Aged rum's caramel and vanilla characteristics complement the malty flavor of English breakfast tea.
- Adjust the syrup slightly depending on the sweetness of the rum and tea syrup.
- Aromatic bitters provide additional spice and depth but are not necessary.
