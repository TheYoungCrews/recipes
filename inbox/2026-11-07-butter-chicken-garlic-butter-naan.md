Budget butter chicken with garlic butter naan, from Maria (@therealchadli) — Ep. 4 of her "Michelin on a Minimum Wage" series, £2.80 a portion.

## Original

Marinade:
- 2 tbsp Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp ginger and garlic paste

Sauce:
- 10 chicken thighs, chopped
- 2 red onions, finely diced
- 1 can chopped tomatoes
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp turmeric
- 3/4 tbsp chili powder
- 3/4 tbsp salt
- 1 tsp fenugreek
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 150 ml double cream (heavy cream)
- 2 tbsp butter
- Chopped coriander, to garnish

Garlic butter naan:
- Naan bread (store-bought)
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tsp fenugreek

1. Marinate the chicken thighs in the yogurt and marinade spices for at least 30 minutes, preferably overnight.
2. Grill the marinated chicken on a baking tray at 210°C (410°F) for 25 minutes.
3. Fry the chopped onions until golden brown, then add the chopped tomatoes and 3/4 of the can filled with water. Mix well, add the sauce spices, and cook 10 minutes on low heat.
4. Let the sauce cool slightly, then blend until smooth.
5. Return the sauce to the pan with the cooked chicken. Stir in the cream and butter, finish with fresh coriander, and cook 10 more minutes on low heat.
6. For the naan: brush store-bought naan with the melted butter mixed with garlic and fenugreek (warming step not specified in the caption).
