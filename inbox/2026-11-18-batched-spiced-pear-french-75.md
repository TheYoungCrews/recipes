Julianna McIntosh (@join_jules) batches this French 75 ahead for holiday hosting — gin, Meyer lemon and pear liqueur with a honeyed spiced pear syrup, topped with champagne to order.

## Original

Batch (approx. 8):

- 1.5 cups London dry gin
- 3/4 cup fresh Meyer lemon juice
- 1/2 cup pear liqueur, or pear juice
- 1/2 cup spiced pear syrup (below)
- 1/4 cup filtered water
- Champagne, to top

1. Add everything except the champagne to a pitcher (or bottle).
2. Chill in the fridge or freezer until properly cold.
3. Fill a flute or coupe 3/4 full with the batch and top with champagne.

For two, made to order: 3 oz gin, 1.5 oz Meyer lemon juice, 1 oz pear liqueur or juice, 1 oz spiced pear syrup — shake with ice, double strain into a chilled flute or coupe, top with champagne.

Spiced pear syrup:

- 1 cup honey, or sugar of choice
- 1 cup filtered water
- 1 pear, chopped
- 2 slices ginger
- 2 cinnamon sticks
- 4–5 cloves
- 1 nutmeg pod
- Splash of vanilla extract

1. Put everything except the vanilla in a pot over medium heat.
2. Simmer 20–30 minutes, until the honey and water take on a syrup consistency.
3. Cool completely, strain, then stir in the vanilla.
4. Keeps in the fridge 2–4 weeks.
