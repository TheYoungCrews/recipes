# Vanilla Tea Rum Cream

**Yield:** 1 cocktail
**Profile:** Creamy, cozy, vanilla-forward, lightly spiced

### Ingredients
- 1½ oz El Dorado rum
- ½ oz Licor 43
- ¼–½ oz homemade English breakfast tea simple syrup
- 1½ oz whole milk or half-and-half
- Tiny pinch of sea salt (amount not given)
- Freshly grated nutmeg for garnish (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Chill a coupe glass.
2. Combine rum, Licor 43, tea syrup, milk, and salt in a cocktail shaker.
3. Add ice and shake vigorously until thoroughly chilled and lightly frothy.
4. Double-strain into the chilled coupe.
5. Garnish with freshly grated nutmeg.

### Recipe Notes
- Licor 43 adds vanilla, citrus, and warm spice notes that pair beautifully with black tea.
- Start with ¼ oz tea syrup because Licor 43 already contributes sweetness.
- Whole milk produces a lighter cocktail, while half-and-half creates a richer, dessert-like texture.
- Serve immediately while cold and frothy.
