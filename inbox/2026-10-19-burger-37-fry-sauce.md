# Burger 37 Fry Sauce

Serves 6–8
Yield: approximately 1¾ cups

Scaled to one-quarter of the original recipe provided by VT Dining Services.

### Ingredients

- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 4 oz ketchup by weight (113 g; approximately ⅓ cup plus 1 tablespoon)
- 2¼ teaspoons white vinegar
- Scant ½ teaspoon black pepper (exactly ¼ + ⅛ + 1/16 teaspoon)
- 1⅛ teaspoons salt (see note)
- ¼ cup BBQ sauce
- 1½ teaspoons jalapeño extract (see note)

### Instructions

1. Combine all ingredients in a mixing bowl and mix thoroughly.
2. Refrigerate until ready to serve. Serve with fries or burgers.

### Notes

- The salt quantity preserves the original recipe’s ratio. For a less salty starting point, begin with ½ teaspoon and add more to taste.
- The original recipe specifically calls for jalapeño extract, but does not identify a brand or concentration. A different product may require adjusting the amount.
