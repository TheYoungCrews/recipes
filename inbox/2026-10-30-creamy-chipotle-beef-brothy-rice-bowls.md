Steps:
1. Rinse and cook the white rice.
2. Heat oil in a Dutch oven and sear the cubed chuck roast until browned; remove and set aside.
3. In the same pot, sauté the quartered onion and garlic.
4. Stir in the tomato paste, chipotle peppers in adobo, paprika, cumin, oregano, and salt.
5. Pour in the beef bone broth and return the beef to the pot.
6. Cover and braise in the oven until the beef is tender.
7. Remove the beef and shred it slightly.
8. Stir the heavy cream into the braising broth to make a creamy sauce.
9. Serve the beef and creamy broth over the white rice, topped with avocado, pickled red onions, jalapeño, cilantro, and a squeeze of lime.
