# Fitzgerald Cynar Sour

**Category:** Cocktails
**Servings:** 1 cocktail
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes

### Description
A sophisticated bourbon sour with earthy, herbal bitterness and bright citrus. Old Fitzgerald's smooth, wheated bourbon character pairs with Cynar's botanical depth and Aperol's bittersweet orange notes. A touch of maple syrup rounds out the flavors without overpowering the whiskey.

### Ingredients
- 1½ oz Old Fitzgerald bourbon
- ½ oz Cynar
- ½ oz Aperol
- ¾ oz freshly squeezed lemon juice
- ¼ oz maple syrup (optional, for additional sweetness)
- Ice (amount not given)
- Orange or lemon twist, for garnish (optional) (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Add bourbon, Cynar, Aperol, lemon juice, and optional maple syrup to a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill the shaker with ice.
3. Shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds until thoroughly chilled.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe glass.
5. Garnish with an orange or lemon twist, if desired.

### Notes
- For a drier cocktail, omit the maple syrup.
- For a rounder, sweeter cocktail, add the ¼ oz maple syrup.
- Cynar contributes earthy, bittersweet complexity, while Aperol adds citrus brightness.
- Best served straight up in a chilled coupe.

**Flavor Profile:** Bittersweet, herbal, citrusy, earthy, bourbon-forward.
