Nobu Matsuhisa's signature miso black cod — sablefish cured in a sweet white-miso marinade, then seared and finished in the oven — in Umami Girl's adaptation from Nobu: The Cookbook.

## Original

Ingredients:
- 4 pieces black cod (sablefish) fillet, about 6 ounces (170 g) each, skin on
- ½ cup (120 ml) dry sake
- ½ cup (120 ml) mirin
- ½ cup (120 g) white miso paste
- 6 tbsp (75 g) sugar
- 1 tbsp (15 ml) safflower oil

Steps:
1. Pat fillets dry with paper towels.
2. Combine sake and mirin in a small pot. Bring to a boil and boil for 30 seconds to cook off alcohol.
3. Off the heat, whisk in miso paste and sugar.
4. Return pot to medium heat and simmer, whisking constantly, for one minute.
5. Pour marinade into a large baking dish and cool to room temperature.
6. Place fish, skin-side up, into baking dish with marinade. Cover and refrigerate for 24 to 48 hours.
7. Preheat oven to 400°F.
8. Heat a 12-inch cast-iron skillet over high heat on the stovetop.
9. Gently wipe off any excess marinade clinging to the fish.
10. Carefully pour the oil into the pan.
11. Arrange the fish fillets skin-side-up in an uncrowded single layer.
12. Cook until the bottom of the fish browns and blackens in spots, about 3 minutes.
13. Flip and continue cooking until the skin side is browned, 2 to 3 minutes.
14. Transfer to the oven and bake for 5 to 10 minutes, until fish is opaque and flakes easily. Remove any pin bones from the fish with tweezers before serving (they'll be running in a line down the center of the fillet).

## Our version

Same as original. For the sear, avocado oil stands in for the safflower oil (high smoke point, what's on hand).

- 2026-11-07: web recipe (Umami Girl, adapted from Nobu: The Cookbook). The 30 minutes is active cooking time — the fish must marinate 24 to 48 hours ahead (up to 72 works). Source notes the same amount of marinade covers double the fish.
