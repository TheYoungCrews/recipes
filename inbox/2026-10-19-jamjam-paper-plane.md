# JamJam Paper Plane

Yield: 1 cocktail

Ingredients:
- 1 oz JamJam Gin
- 3/4 oz Aperol
- 3/4 oz Amaro Nonino
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice

Instructions:
1. Add all ingredients to a cocktail shaker or mason jar with ice.
2. Shake vigorously for about 15 seconds, until well chilled.
3. Strain into a chilled coupe or Nick & Nora glass.
4. Serve immediately.

Notes:
A gin-based riff inspired by the Paper Plane. JamJam Gin brings strawberry, citrus, and herbal notes, while Aperol and Amaro Nonino make the drink bittersweet, bright, and gently complex.
