# Classic Caesar Dressing

**Yield:** About 3–4 tablespoons, enough for 1 romaine heart

### Ingredients
- 1 small garlic clove, finely minced
- 1 anchovy fillet, mashed (or ½ tsp anchovy paste)
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1½ tsp lemon juice, plus more to taste
- 1 egg yolk (or 1 tbsp mayonnaise)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp finely grated Parmesan
- Pinch of freshly ground black pepper (amount not given)
- Optional: tiny dash Worcestershire sauce (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Mash the garlic and anchovy together into a paste in a small bowl.
2. Whisk in the Dijon, lemon juice, and egg yolk (or mayonnaise).
3. Slowly drizzle in the olive oil while whisking continuously until thickened and glossy.
4. Stir in Parmesan and black pepper. Add Worcestershire if using.
5. Taste and adjust lemon or seasoning. Thin with a teaspoon of cold water if needed.
6. Toss with one chopped romaine heart until evenly coated. Finish with extra Parmesan and freshly cracked black pepper.

**Note:** For a no-raw-egg variation, use mayonnaise in place of the yolk.
