# Casi Cielo Squash Soup

Adapted from Emeril Lagasse’s Butternut Squash Soup, with peanut oil, chipotle pepper flakes, and creamy, crunchy chili toppings.

## Ingredients

- 1 butternut squash, about 2 pounds
- 2 tablespoons peanut oil (used instead of olive oil)
- 1 cup chopped onion
- 1½ teaspoons chopped garlic
- ½ cup thinly sliced carrot
- ½ teaspoon ground cumin
- ½ teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- 1 tablespoon finely minced jalapeño pepper
- ½ teaspoon chipotle pepper flakes (this makes it a bit spicy; start with ¼ teaspoon for less heat)
- 2 cups chicken stock
- ¼ cup heavy cream

## Toppings

- Crème fraîche or Greek yogurt (amount not given)
- Chili oil or chili crunch (I use Trader Joe’s Chili Crunch) (amount not given)
- Pumpkin seeds, optional (amount not given)

## Instructions

1. Cut the squash in half and scoop out the seeds. Peel and cut into 1-inch pieces.
2. In a large pot, heat the peanut oil over medium heat. Add the onion and cook, stirring often, until it begins to brown, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic and cook for about 30 seconds.
3. Add the carrot, cumin, salt, black pepper, and chipotle pepper flakes. Cook for 1 minute, then add the squash, jalapeño, and chicken stock.
4. Bring to a boil, reduce the heat, and simmer for 15–20 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
5. Remove from the heat and purée with an immersion blender until smooth. Alternatively, carefully blend in batches in a blender, allowing steam to escape.
6. Return the soup to the heat, stir in the cream, and adjust the seasonings to taste.
7. Serve topped with a dollop of crème fraîche or Greek yogurt and a drizzle of chili oil or a spoonful of Trader Joe’s Chili Crunch. Add pumpkin seeds if desired.

Heat note: The ½ teaspoon of chipotle pepper flakes makes this soup a bit spicy, especially with the jalapeño and chili topping. Start with ¼ teaspoon if you prefer it milder.
