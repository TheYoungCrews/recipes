Equal-parts split-base rum, Punt e Mes, and Cynar stirred cocktail (@theeducatedbarfly).

## Original
- 0.75 oz Smith & Cross Traditional Jamaica Rum
- 0.75 oz Aged Dark Rum
- 0.75 oz Punt e Mes
- 0.75 oz Cynar

1. Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice (no bitters).
2. Stir for 35-40 seconds until well chilled and properly diluted.
3. Strain into a chilled glass and garnish with an expressed lime twist.

- [x] 0.75 oz Smith & Cross rum
