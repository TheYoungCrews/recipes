Wayne Cafariella's (@drinkwithwayne) home replica of a carbonated agave cocktail from Tequila Ocho's Casa de Ocho — Midori, pineapple, and coconut water, force-carbonated.

## Original
> Faithful decode of the caption, which gives the full spec. The post is tagged #ad (Tequila Ocho).

**Ingredients**
- 2 oz Tequila Ocho Plata (any blanco tequila works)
- 1 oz Midori
- 1 oz pineapple puree
- 1/4 oz agave syrup
- 3/4 oz Supasawa or lime juice
- 6 oz coconut water
- Ice

**Steps**
1. Combine all ingredients and chill.
2. Force carbonate at 50 psi.
3. Serve in a highball glass over ice.
