The Educated Barfly's gussied-up Washington Apple — the nightclub classic rebuilt on bonded bourbon and rye for fall.

## Original

Ingredients:
- 1 oz bonded bourbon
- 1 oz bonded rye
- 3/4 oz apple cider
- 1/2 oz cranberry syrup
- 1/2 oz lemon juice
- thin apple slice, for garnish
- ice

Steps:
1. Add the bourbon, rye, apple cider, cranberry syrup, and lemon juice to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake vigorously.
3. Fine-strain into a rocks glass filled with fresh ice.
4. Garnish with a thin apple slice.
