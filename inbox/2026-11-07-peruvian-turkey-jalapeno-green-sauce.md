Turkey thighs in a Peruvian aji pepper marinade with a jalapeño green sauce, from Nacho Fuentes (@nachocookin).

## Original

Turkey:
- 4–5 lbs turkey thighs
- 2–3 fresh or frozen aji amarillo peppers
- 2–3 fresh or frozen aji panca peppers
- Zest + juice of 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp liquid beef tallow
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp cumin
- ½ tsp oregano
- 7 cloves garlic
- Salt & black pepper, to taste

Jalapeño Green Sauce:
- 2–3 jalapeños (adjust for heat)
- 1 cup fresh cilantro
- 2–3 garlic cloves
- ½ cup Greek yogurt
- 2–3 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste

Steps:
1. Add the aji amarillo, aji panca, lemon zest and juice, soy sauce, liquid beef tallow, paprika, cumin, oregano, garlic, salt and pepper to a blender. Blend until smooth.
2. Coat the turkey thighs evenly with the marinade. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, or overnight.
3. Preheat your grill or oven to 375°F. Grill over indirect heat or place the turkey on a rack over a baking sheet.
4. Cook until the thickest part of the thighs reaches 175–185°F, about 45–60 minutes.
5. For crispier skin, finish over direct heat or increase the oven temperature for the last few minutes, watching closely so the marinade doesn't burn.
6. Rest for 10–15 minutes before serving.
7. For the green sauce: blend the jalapeños, cilantro, garlic, Greek yogurt, olive oil, salt and black pepper in a blender or food processor until smooth. Chill and serve.
