# English Breakfast Old Fashioned

**Yield:** 1 cocktail
**Profile:** Spirit-forward, malty, aromatic, subtly sweet

### Ingredients
- 2 oz Old Fitzgerald bourbon
- ¼ oz homemade English breakfast tea simple syrup
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- 1 dash orange bitters (optional)
- Orange peel for garnish (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Combine bourbon, tea syrup, and bitters in a mixing glass.
2. Fill with ice and stir for 20–25 seconds until chilled and properly diluted.
3. Strain into a rocks glass over one large ice cube.
4. Express an orange peel over the drink to release its oils.
5. Drop the peel into the glass and serve.

### Recipe Notes
- English breakfast tea contributes maltiness and subtle tannins, balancing the bourbon's caramel and vanilla notes.
- Orange bitters add citrus complexity without increasing sweetness.
- This is an excellent variation for anyone who enjoys traditional, spirit-forward Old Fashioneds.
