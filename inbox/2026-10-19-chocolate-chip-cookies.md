# Chocolate Chip Cookies

Soft and chewy chocolate chip cookies with lightly crisp edges. This version uses mostly light brown sugar with a little granulated sugar for balance, and slightly less total sugar than the original recipe. No chilling required.

Ingredients
- 1 cup (227 g) butter, melted
- 240 g light brown sugar, packed
- 50 g granulated sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 2 1/2 cups (about 300 g) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon fine sea salt
- 10 oz (283 g) semisweet chocolate chips

Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Melt the butter until just melted; it should not be piping hot.
3. Add the light brown sugar and granulated sugar and stir well to combine.
4. Let the butter-sugar mixture stand for 5 minutes.
5. Add the egg and egg yolk and stir until thoroughly combined.
6. Stir in the vanilla.
7. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt. Stir by hand just until combined; do not overmix.
8. Fold in the chocolate chips.
9. Using a medium cookie scoop (about 1 1/2 tablespoons), portion dough onto the prepared baking sheet, leaving about 2 inches between cookies. Do not chill the dough.
10. Bake for 7 minutes, until the edges are set but the centers still look soft and slightly underbaked.
11. Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for about 5 minutes before transferring to a wire rack to finish cooling.

Notes
- For the thinner, chewier texture, bake the dough immediately rather than refrigerating it.
- The cookies will continue to set as they cool, so resist the urge to bake until the centers look completely done.
- A light-colored baking sheet is ideal to prevent the bottoms from getting overly toasted. If using a darker pan, keep the oven rack around the upper-middle position and make sure the pan cools completely between batches.
- Store completely cooled cookies in an airtight container at room temperature.

Adapted from Kate Dean’s Chewy Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe at I Heart Eating.
