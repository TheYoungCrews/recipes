# Bratwurst Mustard Lentils

A cozy skillet dinner with browned bratwurst, sweet Vidalia onion, and warm lentils finished with Dijon mustard, butter, and sherry vinegar. We made it with the cooked bratwurst from Costco's mixed sausage pack and Trader Joe's precooked lentils, and loved it.

Serves: 2–3
Time: About 25–30 minutes
Creator: ChatGPT, developed with Anna

### Ingredients

- 3–4 fully cooked bratwurst

- 1 package Trader Joe's precooked lentils

- 1 small Vidalia onion, thinly sliced

- 2 garlic cloves, minced

- 1 tablespoon olive oil

- ⅓ cup chicken broth or water, plus more as needed

- 2 teaspoons Dijon mustard

- 1 tablespoon butter

- 1 teaspoon sherry vinegar, plus more to taste

- Black pepper and salt, to taste (amount not given)

- Brown mustard, for serving (amount not given)

- Optional: 1 small carrot, finely diced; a pinch of thyme; parsley; or a handful of spinach

### Instructions

1. Brown the bratwurst. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Pat the bratwurst dry and brown gently, turning occasionally, for about 5–7 minutes. Transfer to a plate; they will finish heating with the lentils.

2. Cook the onion. Add the Vidalia onion and optional carrot to the same skillet. Cook for 7–9 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and lightly golden. Add the garlic and optional thyme and cook for 30 seconds.

3. Add the lentils. Gently break apart the lentils in the pan. Stir in the broth, Dijon, and black pepper, scraping up the browned bits.

4. Warm together. Nestle the bratwurst into the lentils. Cover and simmer gently for 5–8 minutes, until everything is hot throughout, following the bratwurst package's heating instructions. Add a splash of broth or water if the lentils look dry. If using spinach, stir it in toward the end to wilt.

5. Finish. Remove from the heat and gently stir the butter and 1 teaspoon sherry vinegar into the lentils. Taste before adding salt, then adjust with more vinegar or mustard if desired. Sprinkle with parsley if using.

6. Serve. Spoon the lentils into shallow bowls and top with bratwurst. Serve brown mustard on the side.

Notes: Let the Vidalia onion get soft and lightly golden for sweetness. Sherry vinegar balances the richness without adding more sweetness. Optional accompaniments include pickles, sauerkraut, or a simple green salad.
