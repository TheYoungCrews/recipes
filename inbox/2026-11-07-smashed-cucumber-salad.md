Pai huang gua, the Sichuan smashed cucumber salad, in America's Test Kitchen's version — cucumbers smashed so the craggy edges grab a garlicky black-vinegar dressing.

## Original

Ingredients:
- 2 (14-ounce) English cucumbers
- 1½ tsp kosher salt
- 4 tsp Chinese black vinegar
- 1 tsp garlic, minced to paste
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp sesame seeds, toasted

Steps:
1. Trim and discard ends from cucumbers. Cut each cucumber crosswise into three equal lengths. Place pieces in a large zipper-lock bag and seal. Using a small skillet or rolling pin, firmly but gently smash cucumbers until flattened and split lengthwise into 3 to 4 spears each. Tear spears into rough 1- to 1½-inch pieces and transfer to a colander set in a large bowl. Toss cucumbers with salt and let stand for at least 15 minutes or up to 30 minutes.
2. While cucumbers sit, whisk vinegar and garlic together in a small bowl; let stand for at least 5 minutes or up to 15 minutes.
3. Whisk soy sauce, oil, and sugar into the vinegar mixture until sugar has dissolved. Transfer cucumbers to a medium bowl and discard any extracted liquid. Add dressing and sesame seeds to cucumbers and toss to combine. Serve immediately.

Same as original.
