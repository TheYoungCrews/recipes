Coastal batch negroni riff built on basil-infused Murrell's Row Jam Jam gin, Dolin Chambéryzette strawberry vermouth, St-Germain, and a touch of Campari.

## Original
- 12 oz Basil-Infused Murrell's Row Jam Jam Gin (1.25 oz single serve)
- 8 oz Dolin Chambéryzette Strawberry Vermouth (1 oz single serve)
- 4 oz St-Germain Elderflower Liqueur (0.5 oz single serve)
- 1 oz Campari (1 barspoon / 0.125 oz single serve)
- 4 oz Cold Filtered Water (0.25 oz single serve)

1. Steep 15-20 slapped fresh basil leaves in the gin for 45-60 minutes, then fine-strain.
2. Combine infused gin, Chambéryzette, St-Germain, Campari, and pre-dilution water in a bottle and chill.
3. Pour over a large ice cube and garnish with a fresh basil leaf.
