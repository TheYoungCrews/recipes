# Campari Nonino Cloud

DESCRIPTION
A rich, bittersweet, herbal, citrus-free amaro cocktail softened with aquafaba.

INGREDIENTS
- 1½ oz Amaro Nonino
- ½ oz Campari
- ½ oz Noilly Prat dry vermouth
- ¾ oz aquafaba

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker without ice.
2. Dry shake vigorously for about 20 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake for another 15 seconds.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
5. Let the foam settle before garnishing.

GARNISH
- Orange peel, or a few drops of Campari on the foam (amount not given)
