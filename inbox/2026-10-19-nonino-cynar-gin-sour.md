# Nonino Cynar Gin Sour

DESCRIPTION
An earthy, bittersweet gin sour with Cynar, lemon, and a small amount of Amaro Nonino.

INGREDIENTS
- 1½ oz gin
- ¾ oz Cynar
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¼ oz Amaro Nonino

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake until well chilled.
3. Strain into a chilled coupe and serve up.

GARNISH
- Lemon twist, optional (amount not given)
