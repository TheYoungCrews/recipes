# Lemony Garlic Chicken

**Source:** Original recipe developed with ChatGPT
**added_by:** anna
**via:** chatgpt
**Serves:** 4
**Time:** Approximately 3 hours
**Method:** Dutch oven, convection oven at 250°F

## Description
Tender, slow-braised chicken thighs nestled in silky onions, bell peppers, and garlic with a Sauvignon Blanc and Dijon broth. Finished with fresh lemon, aged sherry vinegar, and additional garlic for a bright, savory sauce.

## Ingredients
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 1 medium onion, sliced
- 1–2 bell peppers, sliced
- 6–8 cloves garlic, divided
- 1–2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste (amount not given)
- ½ tsp dried thyme or oregano (optional)
- Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional) (amount not given)
- ½ cup Sauvignon Blanc
- ¾ cup chicken broth, plus additional broth as needed
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- Juice of ½ lemon, plus more to taste
- ½–1 tsp lemon zest (optional)
- 1 tsp aged sherry vinegar, plus more to taste
- 1 tbsp butter (optional, for finishing)

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat and sear the chicken
Preheat convection oven to 250°F. Pat chicken thighs dry and generously season both sides with salt and black pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Sear chicken for approximately 3 minutes on the first side and 2 minutes on the second, until golden brown. Transfer chicken to a shallow plate or container. Allow to cool briefly, then cover and refrigerate while the vegetables braise.

### 2. Sauté the vegetables
Reduce heat to medium. Add sliced onion and bell peppers to the Dutch oven, adding another splash of olive oil if necessary. Season with a pinch of salt and sauté for 5–7 minutes, stirring occasionally and scraping up the browned bits from the chicken. Add 5–6 cloves of minced or sliced garlic, reserving 1–2 cloves for finishing. Stir in thyme or oregano and red pepper flakes, if using. Cook for 30–60 seconds until fragrant.

### 3. Deglaze and prepare the braising liquid
Pour in ½ cup Sauvignon Blanc. Scrape the bottom of the Dutch oven thoroughly to release the browned bits. Allow wine to simmer for 2–3 minutes. Stir in ¾ cup chicken broth and 1 tbsp Dijon mustard. Bring to a gentle simmer. Reserve the lemon juice and sherry vinegar for the finishing sauce.

### 4. Slow-braise the vegetables
Cover the Dutch oven and transfer to the 250°F convection oven. Braise for approximately 90 minutes. Check after 60 minutes to ensure sufficient liquid remains. If necessary, add another ¼ cup chicken broth. The onions, peppers, and garlic should become very soft and silky.

### 5. Add the chicken
After 90 minutes, remove the Dutch oven from the oven. Nestle the refrigerated chicken thighs into the vegetables and braising liquid, adding any accumulated juices. Spoon some vegetables and sauce over the chicken. Cover and return to the oven for approximately 60 minutes, until the chicken is fully cooked and tender. Chicken must reach at least 165°F internally.

### 6. Reduce the sauce
Remove the Dutch oven lid. Continue cooking uncovered for another 20–30 minutes at 250°F convection, allowing the sauce to concentrate slightly. Shorten this step if the sauce has already reduced sufficiently.

### 7. Finish with fresh garlic, lemon, and sherry vinegar
Remove the chicken and set aside. Place the Dutch oven on the stovetop over medium-low heat. Finely mince or grate the remaining 1–2 garlic cloves and add them to the sauce. Cook gently for 1–2 minutes. Turn off the heat and stir in:
- Juice of ½ lemon
- 1 tsp aged sherry vinegar
- ½–1 tsp lemon zest, if using
- Freshly cracked black pepper
- 1 tbsp butter, if desired

Taste and adjust seasoning. Add additional lemon juice for brightness, sherry vinegar for acidity, or salt to enhance the flavors. Return chicken to the Dutch oven and spoon the sauce and vegetables over the top.

- **Wine:** Sauvignon Blanc is preferred over Chardonnay for its crisp acidity and brighter flavor.
- **Garlic:** Dividing the garlic creates two layers of flavor: mellow, sweet garlic from the slow braise and fresh, aromatic garlic in the finished sauce.
- **Acidity:** Adding lemon and sherry vinegar at the end preserves their brightness.
- **Texture:** Braising the vegetables before adding the chicken prevents boneless thighs from overcooking during the full three-hour process.
- **Sauce:** For a richer, silkier finish, swirl in 1 tbsp butter after turning off the heat.
- **Serving suggestions:** Excellent over mashed potatoes, creamy polenta, couscous, rice, or with crusty bread to soak up the sauce.
- **Storage:** Refrigerate leftovers promptly in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days.

**Flavor profile:** Bright, lemony, garlicky, savory, wine-forward, and delicately herbaceous.
