# Old Fitzgerald Paper Plane

**Category:** Cocktails
**Servings:** 1 cocktail
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes

### Description
A classic modern cocktail featuring equal parts bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and fresh lemon juice. This version uses Old Fitzgerald wheated bourbon, creating a smooth, balanced drink with bright citrus, bittersweet orange, and layered herbal complexity.

### Ingredients
- ¾ oz Old Fitzgerald bourbon
- ¾ oz Aperol
- ¾ oz Amaro Nonino
- ¾ oz freshly squeezed lemon juice
- Ice (amount not given)
- Lemon twist, for garnish (optional) (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Add bourbon, Aperol, Amaro Nonino, and fresh lemon juice to a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill the shaker with ice.
3. Shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds until thoroughly chilled.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe glass.
5. Garnish with a lemon twist, if desired.

### Notes
- The Paper Plane is an equal-parts cocktail, making it easy to scale.
- Aperol contributes bittersweet orange flavor and warm color.
- Amaro Nonino adds herbal, citrus, and lightly spiced complexity.
- Fresh lemon juice provides acidity to balance sweetness.
- Old Fitzgerald's soft, wheated bourbon profile gives this variation a smooth finish.
- Serve straight up in a chilled coupe.

**Flavor Profile:** Citrusy, bittersweet, herbal, smooth, balanced.
