# Earl Grey Rum Cocktail

A silky, lightly sweet, tea-forward cocktail with warm caramel and vanilla notes from El Dorado 8 Year rum, citrus and spice from Licor 43, and fragrant Earl Grey. Shaken and served up in a chilled coupe.

**Yield:** 1 cocktail  
**Method:** Shaken, served up

## Ingredients
- 1½ oz El Dorado 8 Year Rum
- ½ oz Licor 43
- 2 oz strongly brewed Earl Grey tea, chilled
- 1 oz whole milk
- Tiny pinch of salt (amount not given)
- Ice, for shaking (amount not given)
- Orange peel, for garnish (amount not given)

## Instructions
1. Place a coupe glass in the freezer to chill.
2. Brew concentrated Earl Grey: steep 2 tea bags in 4 oz hot water for 4 minutes. Remove the bags and chill the tea completely. (This makes enough tea for two cocktails.)
3. Add 1½ oz rum, ½ oz Licor 43, 2 oz chilled Earl Grey tea, 1 oz whole milk, and a tiny pinch of salt to a cocktail shaker.
4. Fill the shaker with ice and shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds.
5. Double strain into the chilled coupe glass, without ice.
6. Express the oils from a strip of orange peel over the drink and garnish.

- The ½ oz Licor 43 adds enough vanilla-citrus sweetness without overpowering the Earl Grey.
- For a sweeter drink, increase Licor 43 to ¾ oz.
- For best texture, use whole milk and make sure the tea is cold before shaking.
- Orange peel brightens the bergamot and vanilla notes, but a light dusting of nutmeg also works.
