@lattes.and.lilies' fall coffee syrup — toasted pecans steeped in maple syrup, with a bonus batch of maple-roasted pecans from the leftovers.

## Original

Ingredients:
- 2 cups chopped pecans
- 1 1/2 cups water
- 1 cup maple syrup
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- sea salt (for the leftover pecans)

Steps:
1. Put the pecans in a dry pan over low to medium heat and move them around for 3-5 minutes, until toasty and fragrant.
2. Add the maple syrup and stir to coat the pecans for another 2-3 minutes, then add the water.
3. Stir to combine and keep at a low simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Let the mixture cool, then transfer to a container with the pecans still in it and rest on the countertop for 4-6 hours.
5. Strain into a glass bottle and store in the fridge for up to 2 weeks.
6. Bonus: spread the maple-soaked pecans on a baking tray, sprinkle with sea salt, and bake at 350°F for 7 minutes. Stir, then bake another 3-7 minutes, watching closely for burning.
