Classic old-fashioned sausage (sawmill) gravy from Sam Merritt at Sugar
Spun Run: four ingredients, 15 minutes, made for biscuits.

## Original

Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, serves 6.

Ingredients
- 1 lb (455 g) pork breakfast sausage (sage flavored recommended)
- 1/4 cup (31 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 cups (590 ml) whole milk
- 1/8 tsp crushed red pepper, optional
- salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

Steps
1. Place sausage in a skillet over medium/high heat.
2. Break up and crumble the sausage as it cooks until completely browned
and no pink remains. Don't drain: keep at least 1 Tbsp, up to 2 Tbsp, of
grease; drain only if there is much more.
3. Sprinkle flour evenly over the sausage. Stir frequently until absorbed
(about 1 minute).
4. Slowly drizzle in the milk, stirring as you pour. Add crushed red pepper
if using.
5. Cook, stirring frequently, until thickened to your liking.
6. Season with salt and pepper and serve warm over biscuits.

Tips from the post: break up flour clumps before adding milk. It looks
runny at first and thickens as it cooks; don't add more flour, just keep
cooking. Whole milk gives the right consistency. Make ahead: refrigerate
overnight, reheat in a skillet over medium-low with a splash of milk.
