Short pasta tossed in whipped ricotta with Calabrian chili butter and Parmigiano — quick, creamy, a little spicy — from The Pasta Table (@thepastatable).

## Original

- 1/2 lb creste di gallo or other short pasta
- 1 1/2 cups whole milk ricotta
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1 tbsp crushed Calabrian chili
- 1/2 cup Parmigiano Reggiano, finely grated
- Pasta water, as needed
- Salt to taste

Steps (from the video; the full written recipe is at the creator's link in bio):
1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water; reserve pasta water before draining.
2. Whip the ricotta until smooth and creamy.
3. Melt the butter with the crushed Calabrian chili to make a chili butter.
4. Spread the whipped ricotta in a warm pan, drizzle over the chili butter and swirl together.
5. Add the drained pasta with a ladle of pasta water and toss until every piece is coated in the creamy sauce, loosening with more pasta water as needed.
6. Fold in the Parmigiano Reggiano, season with salt to taste, and serve.
