Classic gin bramble crowned with our annual April homemade Beaujolais-base blackberry liqueur.

## Original
- 2 oz London Dry Gin
- 0.75 oz Fresh Lemon Juice
- 0.375 oz Simple Syrup
- 0.75 oz Homemade Beaujolais Crème de Mûre

1. Whip shake the gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with a few pieces of pebble ice until melted.
2. Dump into a double old-fashioned glass packed with crushed or pebble ice.
3. Drizzle the homemade Beaujolais Crème de Mûre over the crown.
