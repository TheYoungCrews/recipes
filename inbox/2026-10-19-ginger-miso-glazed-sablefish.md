# Ginger-Miso Glazed Sablefish
time: prep: 15 minutes plus 1–2 hours refrigerated marinating; cook: about 10–17 minutes

A gently roasted, honey-ginger miso black cod glaze made without mirin or sake. This is the final enlarged glaze batch developed for six fillets.

## Ingredients
- 6 sablefish / black cod fillets, about 2½–3 lb total
- 6 tbsp white miso
- 1½ tbsp liquid aminos
- 3 tbsp water
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp plus 1 tsp freshly grated ginger
- 1½ tbsp rice vinegar
- 1½ tsp toasted sesame oil
- 3 garlic cloves, minced or grated

## Steps
1. Remove fish from any vacuum-sealed packaging before thawing safely in the refrigerator. Pat thawed fillets dry.
2. Whisk miso, liquid aminos, water, honey, ginger, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and garlic until evenly blended.
3. Set aside about ¼ cup of the CLEAN glaze before it contacts raw fish to use for brushing near the end.
4. Place the fillets in two bags or shallow covered containers with remaining glaze. Refrigerate 1–2 hours, three fillets per bag if using two bags.
5. Preheat oven to 425°F. Set fish skin-side down on a suitable lined baking tray or rack over a tray.
6. Roast roughly 10–15 minutes depending on thickness, until the fish reaches 145°F internally.
7. Brush with the reserved clean glaze near the end, then broil briefly 1–2 minutes for caramelization, watching continuously so the honey and miso don't burn. Confirm safe doneness.
