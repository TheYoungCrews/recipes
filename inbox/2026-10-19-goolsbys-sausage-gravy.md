# Goolsby’s Sausage Gravy

**Yield:** About 4 servings  
**Prep time:** 5 minutes  
**Cook time:** 15–20 minutes

## Ingredients
- 5–6 Goolsby's frozen sausage patties (approximately 9–11 oz)
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 2 cups whole milk, plus more as needed
- 1–2 tablespoons butter, only if needed
- ½–¾ teaspoon freshly cracked black pepper, to taste
- Pinch of garlic powder (optional) (amount not given)
- Pinch of rubbed sage (optional) (amount not given)
- Salt, to taste (amount not given)

## Instructions
1. **Cook the sausage.** Place frozen sausage patties in a skillet over medium-low heat and cook according to package directions, turning as needed. If the sausage is raw, cook to an internal temperature of 160°F.
2. **Crumble and brown.** Break the sausage into small pieces using a spatula. Let it brown slightly for extra flavor.
3. **Check the fat.** Leave about 2 tablespoons of rendered sausage fat in the skillet. If there isn't enough, add a little butter to make up the difference.
4. **Make the roux.** Sprinkle the flour over the sausage and fat. Stir continuously for 1–2 minutes to cook off the raw flour flavor without browning it too much.
5. **Add milk gradually.** Pour in about ½ cup of milk, stirring until smooth, then gradually add the remaining milk.
6. **Simmer.** Cook for 4–6 minutes, stirring often, until the gravy is creamy and thickened.
7. **Season.** Stir in black pepper and, if desired, a pinch of garlic powder and sage. Taste before adding salt, since Goolsby's sausage is already seasoned.
8. **Adjust and serve.** Thin with a splash of milk if needed. Spoon generously over warm, split biscuits.

## Recipe Notes
- Don't drain all the sausage drippings; they give the gravy its signature savory flavor.
- Whole milk produces the richest, most velvety texture.
- The gravy thickens as it sits. Stir in a splash of milk when reheating.
- Keep the seasoning simple so the flavor of the Goolsby's sausage shines.
