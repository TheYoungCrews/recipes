Crispy caramelized beef and fluffy rice in a rich coconut curry broth,
finished with cucumber, herbs, and chili crunch. Creator:
@chelseasmessyapron.

## Original
**Ingredients**
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 1 pound lean ground beef (93/7)
- 1-1/2 tablespoons finely minced ginger, divided
- 1-1/2 tablespoons finely minced garlic, divided
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1/4 cup chicken broth
- 2-1/2 tablespoons soy sauce (regular, not lite)
- 2 teaspoons white sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon toasted sesame oil, plus more for topping
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- 2 tablespoons red curry paste
- 1 teaspoon yellow curry powder
- 1 can (13.66 oz) coconut milk
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce (regular, not lite)
- 1 large lime, divided
- 1/4 cup finely chopped cilantro, plus more for topping
- Toasted sesame seeds
- 2 small Persian cucumbers, diced
- 4 green onions, thinly sliced
- Chili crunch (optional)

**Steps**
1. Rice: boil generously salted water, add rice, boil 10 minutes until
tender. Drain, return to the dry pot, cover and steam until serving.
2. Beef: heat coconut oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add
ground beef and leave undisturbed a few minutes to develop browned
spots. Break up and cook, stirring only occasionally, until browned
all over.
3. Sauce: shake cornstarch and 1/4 cup chicken broth smooth in a jar.
Add soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, and white pepper; shake again.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add 1 tablespoon each garlic and ginger;
cook 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the sauce; stir 1-2 minutes
until thickened and caramelized onto the beef. Remove to a plate and
cover.
5. Broth: same skillet over low heat. Cook remaining 1/2 tablespoon
each garlic and ginger 20 seconds. Stir in red curry paste and curry
powder; cook 1 minute. Pour in coconut milk, scraping up the browned
bits; whisk smooth. Add 1 cup chicken broth; boil 1 minute, then
reduce to low. Stir in 1 tablespoon soy sauce, juice of 1/2 lime, and
cilantro.
6. Assemble: divide rice among 3-4 bowls, add beef alongside, ladle
hot broth around everything. Top with cucumber, sesame seeds, green
onions, extra cilantro, and a light drizzle of sesame oil. Add chili
crunch for heat if desired. Serve with lime wedges.

## Our version
Weeknight version (30 min or less, Dutch oven, more lime/herbs/umami,
high-protein): brown the beef hard in the Dutch oven, undisturbed, for
the caramelized edges. Same cornstarch slurry but liquid aminos
instead of soy sauce. Build the broth in the same pot after the beef
comes out. Finish each bowl with extra lime juice, double the
cilantro, and sambal oelek as the chili paste for heat.
