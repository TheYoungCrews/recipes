# Pumpkin Chocolate Pecan Bread

**Source:** Original recipe developed with ChatGPT
**added_by:** anna
**via:** chatgpt
**Serves:** 8–10 slices
**Prep Time:** 10–15 minutes
**Cook Time:** 40–50 minutes
**Total Time:** 50–65 minutes

## Description
A moist, lightly sweet pumpkin bread made with Kodiak Protein Power Cakes mix, whole milk yogurt, warm fall spices, fresh ginger, dark chocolate chips, and chopped pecans. Designed to be more breakfast-friendly than dessert-like, with extra protein and a tender crumb.

## Ingredients
- 2 cups Kodiak Cakes Protein Power Cakes mix (Buttermilk)
- 1 cup pumpkin purée (not pumpkin pie filling)
- 2 large eggs
- ⅓ cup maple syrup or honey
- ¼ cup Bellwether Farms A2 organic whole milk yogurt
- 2–3 tbsp milk, plus more as needed
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
- 1–1½ tsp finely grated fresh ginger
- ⅓ cup dark chocolate chips
- ⅓ cup chopped pecans
- Optional: 1–2 tbsp olive oil or melted butter for extra richness

## Instructions
1. **Preheat the oven:** Set oven to 350°F. Grease a loaf pan or line it with parchment paper.
2. **Mix the wet ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk together pumpkin purée, maple syrup or honey, whole milk yogurt, 2–3 tbsp milk, vanilla extract, fresh ginger, and optional oil or melted butter.
3. **Adjust sweetness and spice:** Taste the wet mixture before adding the eggs. Adjust maple syrup, ginger, or spices according to preference.
4. **Add eggs:** Whisk in both eggs until fully incorporated.
5. **Add dry ingredients:** Fold in the Kodiak Power Cakes mix and pumpkin pie spice until just combined. Avoid overmixing.
6. **Check consistency:** The batter should be thick but scoopable, similar to banana bread batter. If too stiff, add milk 1 tbsp at a time.
7. **Fold in mix-ins:** Gently incorporate the dark chocolate chips and chopped pecans.
8. **Bake:** Transfer batter to the prepared loaf pan and smooth the top. Bake for approximately 40–50 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out without wet batter.
9. **Cool:** Allow the bread to cool in the pan for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
10. **Serve:** Slice once mostly cooled. Enjoy plain, warmed, or with a little butter.

- **Yogurt substitution:** Bellwether Farms whole milk yogurt replaces Greek yogurt 1:1. Because it is thinner, begin with less milk.
- **Fresh ginger:** Adds brightness and warmth. Use 1 tsp for a subtle ginger flavor or 1½ tsp for a more pronounced flavor.
- **Sweetness:** This recipe is intentionally less sweet than traditional pumpkin bread. The dark chocolate chips provide additional sweetness.
- **Texture:** Avoid overmixing the Kodiak batter, which can produce a dense or rubbery loaf.
- **Mix-ins:** Pecans and chocolate chips are folded into the batter. No streusel or additional topping.
- **Storage:** Store airtight at room temperature for 2 days, refrigerate for up to 5 days, or freeze individual slices for longer storage.
- **Doneness:** Baking time may vary with loaf pan size and oven. Check that the center is fully set.
