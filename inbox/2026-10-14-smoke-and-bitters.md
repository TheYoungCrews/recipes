Smoky, bitter stirred agave and amaro cocktail from The Hunt + Alpine Club (@theeducatedbarfly).

## Original
- 1 oz Blanco Tequila
- 0.5 oz Mezcal
- 1 oz Cynar
- 0.5 oz Fernet-Branca

1. Combine all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice and stir for 35 seconds.
2. Strain into a chilled glass and express a grapefruit twist over top.

- [x] 1 oz additive-free blanco tequila

- [x] 1 oz Cynar
