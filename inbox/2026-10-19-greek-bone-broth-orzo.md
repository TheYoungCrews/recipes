# Greek Bone Broth Orzo

**Serves:** 4  
**Total Time:** 30 minutes

## Description
A cozy, lemony Greek-inspired orzo dish cooked directly in savory bone broth for extra flavor. Finished with cherry tomatoes, tender greens, feta, and fresh lemon, this one-pot recipe is creamy without needing cream.

## Ingredients
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small yellow onion, finely chopped
- 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup dry orzo
- 2½ cups chicken or beef bone broth
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried dill (optional)
- Zest and juice of ½ lemon
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 cups chopped spinach or kale
- ½ cup crumbled feta, plus extra for topping
- Salt and black pepper, to taste (amount not given)

### Optional Add-Ins
- Cooked shredded chicken or rotisserie chicken (amount not given)
- Chickpeas or white beans (amount not given)
- Chopped Kalamata olives (amount not given)
- Fresh parsley or mint (amount not given)

## Instructions
1. **Sauté the aromatics:** Heat olive oil in a medium pot or deep skillet over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and sauté for 3–4 minutes until softened. Stir in garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
2. **Toast the orzo:** Add dry orzo directly to the pot. Stir continuously for 1–2 minutes to lightly toast the pasta.
3. **Add the broth and seasonings:** Pour in the bone broth. Add oregano, dill (if using), lemon zest, and a pinch of salt and pepper. Stir well and bring to a gentle boil.
4. **Simmer the orzo:** Reduce heat to low and simmer uncovered for 8–10 minutes, stirring frequently to prevent sticking. The orzo should become tender and absorb most of the broth, developing a creamy, risotto-like consistency. Add a splash of extra broth if needed.
5. **Add vegetables:** Stir in the chopped spinach or kale and halved cherry tomatoes. Cook for another 2–3 minutes, until the greens are wilted and the tomatoes begin to soften.
6. **Finish with feta and lemon:** Remove from heat. Stir in the crumbled feta and lemon juice. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, or lemon as desired.
7. **Serve:** Spoon into bowls and garnish with additional feta, fresh herbs, or Kalamata olives.

## Recipe Notes
- **Bone broth:** Chicken bone broth provides a lighter, more traditional Mediterranean flavor, while beef bone broth creates a richer dish.
- **Consistency:** The orzo will continue absorbing liquid as it rests. Add a splash of warm broth before serving if needed.
- **Protein additions:** Stir in cooked chicken or chickpeas during the last few minutes of cooking to heat through.
- **Greens:** Spinach wilts quickly; kale may need a few extra minutes to soften.
- **Salt:** Bone broth and feta can both be salty, so season conservatively until the end.
- **Storage:** Refrigerate leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3–4 days. Reheat with a splash of broth to restore the creamy consistency.
