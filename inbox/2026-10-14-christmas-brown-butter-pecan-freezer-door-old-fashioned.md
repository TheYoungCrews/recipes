Holiday freezer-door batch built on brown-butter fat-washed high-proof bourbon, spicy rye, toasted pecan syrup, and a trilogy of bitters.

## Original
- 500 ml Brown-Butter Fat-Washed Bourbon (Old Forester 1920 / Old Grand-Dad)
- 200 ml Rittenhouse Rye (100 Proof)
- 100 ml Toasted Pecan Syrup
- 180 ml Cold Filtered Water (pre-dilution)
- Heavy dashes of Angostura, Black Walnut, and Orange bitters
- Tiny pinch of sea salt

1. Fat-wash the bourbon with browned butter, freeze, and strain fine solids.
2. Combine fat-washed bourbon, Rittenhouse Rye, toasted pecan syrup, filtered water, salt, and all three bitters in a 1-liter bottle.
3. Freeze overnight until viscous and pour straight over a large rock.

## Our version
- [x] 500 ml brown-butter fat-washed bourbon (or 1.5 oz single serve)
- [x] 200 ml rittenhouse rye (or 0.5 oz single serve)
- [ ] 100 ml toasted pecan syrup (or 0.25 oz single serve)
- [x] 180 ml cold filtered water (for 1L batch)
- [x] angostura bitters (2 dashes single serve)
- [x] black walnut bitters (2 dashes single serve)
- [x] orange bitters
- [x] sea salt (tiny pinch)

1. For the 1L freezer batch: funnel all ingredients into a bottle, chill in the freezer until sub-zero, and pour over a single large rock with an expressed orange peel.
2. For a single glass: stir 1.5 oz fat-washed bourbon, 0.5 oz Rittenhouse Rye, 0.25 oz pecan syrup, 2 dashes Angostura, and 2 dashes Black Walnut bitters over a large rock.
