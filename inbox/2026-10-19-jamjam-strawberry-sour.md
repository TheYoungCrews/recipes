# JamJam Strawberry Sour

Yield: 1 cocktail

Ingredients:
- 2 oz JamJam Gin
- 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice
- 3/4 oz Chambéryzette
- 3/4 oz aquafaba
- Lemon twist or strawberry, for garnish (amount not given)

Instructions:
1. Add the JamJam Gin, lemon juice, Chambéryzette, and aquafaba to a cocktail shaker or tightly sealed mason jar.
2. Shake vigorously without ice for 15–20 seconds to build foam.
3. Add ice and shake again for another 15–20 seconds, until thoroughly chilled.
4. Double-strain into a coupe or cocktail glass.
5. Garnish with a lemon twist or strawberry.

Notes:
No simple syrup is needed—the Chambéryzette provides enough sweetness while reinforcing the strawberry notes in the gin. Aquafaba is the liquid from a can of chickpeas or garbanzo beans and creates the same silky foam traditionally provided by egg white.
