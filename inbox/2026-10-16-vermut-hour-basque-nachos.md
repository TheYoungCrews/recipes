Lucas Assis's weekly vermut hour: Spanish conservas piled on chips ("basque nachos") with vermouth and an Americano. Creator: @thelucasassis.

## Original
> Assembled from the caption and comments; the video's speech and on-screen text were not reviewed. The source gives no amounts — this is a spread/ritual, not a measured recipe.

**The spread**
- Tortilla chips or other sturdy chips (amount not given)
- Anchovies — Spanish preserved ones; one commenter calls Trader Joe's the best bang for buck (amount not given)
- Guindilla peppers, plus a splash of their pickling juice tossed with the chips (per a commenter) (amount not given)
- Caviar (amount not given)
- Beers alongside (per comments)

**The drink: "Americano perfecto"**
- Campari (amount not given; the classic Americano is equal parts)
- Sweet vermouth — "perfecto" suggests a split of sweet and dry vermouth, but no spec was given (amount not given)
- Soda water to top (amount not given)
- Orange slice or twist (amount not given; classic garnish)

**Steps**
1. Pile the chips with anchovies, guindilla peppers, and caviar. One commenter suggests tossing the peppers and their juice in the chip bag first for max flavor.
2. For the Americano: build Campari and vermouth over ice, top with soda, garnish with orange. (Classic build; the creator's exact "perfecto" spec was not given.)
3. Put on Seu Jorge. The creator does this about once a week with Michelle — serves 2 is inferred from that.
