Braised chuck roast piled on pretzel buns with caramelized onions, provolone, and a roasted garlic horseradish aioli, with the braising liquid as the dip — from The Sapor (@thesapor).

## Original

Beef / au jus:
- 3 lbs chuck roast
- 2 tsp onion powder (for beef)
- 2 tsp garlic powder (for beef)
- Salt and pepper, generously
- 5 cups beef stock
- 6 garlic cloves
- 3 rosemary sprigs
- 2 yellow onions, sliced
- 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp roasted beef bouillon paste
- 1 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp msg (optional)
- Salt to taste
- Avocado oil, for searing

Caramelized onions:
- 5 yellow onions, sliced
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp avocado oil (caption says neutral oil; avocado is the oil already in use here)
- 2 tsp granulated sugar
- Salt to taste

Roasted garlic horseradish aioli:
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 whole head roasted garlic
- 1–1 1/2 tbsp prepared horseradish (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Salt to taste

Extras:
- Pretzel buns
- Garlic butter
- Provolone cheese

Steps (method from the video; braise time and temperature were not stated in the post):
1. Season the chuck roast generously with salt, pepper, and the onion and garlic powders marked for the beef.
2. Sear in avocado oil in a Dutch oven until browned on all sides; remove.
3. Sauté the 2 sliced onions in the same pot, then deglaze with some of the beef stock.
4. Return the beef with the remaining stock, garlic cloves, rosemary, Worcestershire sauce, bouillon paste, remaining onion and garlic powders, msg if using, and salt. Cover and braise until the beef shreds easily (time/temp not given).
5. Meanwhile, caramelize the 5 sliced onions low and slow in the butter and oil with the sugar and salt.
6. Roast the head of garlic until soft; squeeze out the cloves and mash into the mayonnaise with the horseradish, Dijon, lemon juice, Worcestershire sauce, pepper, and salt.
7. Shred the beef. Strain the braising liquid and keep it warm as the au jus.
8. Toast the pretzel buns with garlic butter, pile on beef, caramelized onions, and provolone, and melt the cheese. Spread with the aioli and serve with a bowl of au jus for dipping.
