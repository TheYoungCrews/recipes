Ed Cudlipp's crispy smashed potatoes over lemon-garlic whipped feta, drizzled with chilli-salt hot honey — a sharing platter.

## Original

Ingredients:
- 2 kg baby potatoes
- 6-7 tbsp olive oil, plus 3 tbsp for the whipped feta
- Maldon Chilli Sea Salt, for seasoning, plus 2 tsp for the hot honey and more to finish
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 600 g feta
- 450 g thick Greek yoghurt
- 3 garlic cloves, finely grated
- zest of 2 lemons, plus 3 tbsp lemon juice
- 180 g runny honey
- fresh chives, finely chopped

Steps:
1. Boil the potatoes for 20-30 minutes until fork tender. Drain and leave to steam dry for 5 minutes.
2. Place on a large baking tray and gently smash each potato with the back of a spoon. Drizzle with the 6-7 tbsp olive oil and season with the chilli salt, garlic powder, and smoked paprika.
3. Roast at 200°C fan (about 400°F) for approximately 1 hour until deeply golden and crispy, checking after 40 minutes and rotating the tray if needed.
4. For the whipped feta, blend the feta, Greek yoghurt, 3 tbsp olive oil, garlic, the zest of 1.5 lemons, and the lemon juice until completely smooth and creamy.
5. For the hot honey, gently warm the honey with 2 tsp lightly crushed chilli salt over low heat for 1-2 minutes without letting it boil. Remove from the heat and leave to infuse for a few minutes.
6. Spread the whipped feta over a large serving platter, arrange the crispy potatoes on top, and drizzle generously with the warm hot honey. Finish with the chives, the zest of the remaining half lemon, and an extra sprinkle of chilli salt.

## Our version

If there's no Maldon Chilli Sea Salt in the cupboard, flaky sea salt plus a pinch of chilli flakes stands in. Otherwise same as original.
