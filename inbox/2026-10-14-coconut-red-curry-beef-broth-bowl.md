Warming coconut-red curry broth over rice with sticky honey-soy beef mince and cool cucumber. Creator: @cook.withcourtney.

## Original
**Ingredients**
- 200g beef mince
- 1 large clove garlic
- 1cm piece ginger
- 1 tsp chilli oil or chilli paste
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tsp honey
- White rice
- Sliced cucumber
- 2 spring onions
- Sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp red curry paste
- 1/2 tin coconut milk
- 200ml chicken stock
- 2 kaffir lime leaves (optional)

**Steps**
1. Fry the beef mince in a hot pan with avocado oil until browned.
2. Add the garlic, chilli, and ginger; fry for a few minutes.
3. Add the honey and dark soy sauce; cook until sticky. Set aside.
4. In a separate pan, fry the red curry paste for a few minutes.
5. Add the coconut milk and chicken stock; stir and simmer 3-4 minutes.
6. Bowl up the white rice and beef mince; pour over the broth. Top with cucumber and spring onions.
