# Amaro Nonino Sour

DESCRIPTION
A warm, silky, citrusy, herbal sour with Amaro Nonino as the base spirit.

INGREDIENTS
- 2 oz Amaro Nonino
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¼ oz St-Germain
- ¾ oz aquafaba

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker without ice.
2. Dry shake vigorously for about 20 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake for another 12–15 seconds.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
5. Let the foam settle briefly before garnishing.

GARNISH
- Lemon twist (amount not given)
