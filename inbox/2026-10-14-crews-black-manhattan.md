House Black Manhattan riff splitting Cynar and Punt e Mes over 100-proof rye with Black Walnut and Angostura bitters.

## Original
- 2 oz Rye Whiskey
- 1 oz Averna Amaro
- 1 dash Angostura Bitters
- 1 dash Orange Bitters

1. Stir ingredients with ice and strain into a chilled coupe with a cherry.

## Our version
- [x] 2 oz Rittenhouse Rye
- [x] 0.5 oz Cynar (changed 2026-10-14: was 1 oz Averna)
- [x] 0.5 oz Punt e Mes
- [x] 2 dashes black walnut bitters (changed 2026-10-14: was orange bitters)
- [x] 1 dash angostura bitters

1. Combine rye, Cynar, Punt e Mes, Black Walnut bitters, and Angostura in a mixing glass with dense ice.
2. Stir for 35 seconds, strain into a chilled Nick & Nora or coupe, and garnish with a Luxardo cherry.
