# London Dry Gin Tomatotini

**Yield:** 2 cocktails
**Profile:** Crisp, savory, botanical, lightly briny

### Ingredients
- 4 oz fresh tomato water
- 4 oz London dry gin
- 1 oz dry vermouth
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¼–½ oz olive brine, to taste
- Tiny pinch of sea salt (if tomato water is unsalted) (amount not given)
- Fresh basil, cherry tomatoes, or cocktail olives for garnish (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Chill two coupe or martini glasses.
2. Combine gin, tomato water, dry vermouth, lemon juice, and ¼ oz olive brine in a cocktail shaker.
3. Add ice and shake vigorously for 10–12 seconds.
4. Double-strain into chilled glasses.
5. Garnish with fresh basil or a cocktail olive.

### Recipe Notes
- Start with ¼ oz olive brine and increase to ½ oz for a more savory, dirty-martini-inspired variation.
- For a more delicate, botanical cocktail, omit the brine and garnish with basil and freshly cracked black pepper.
- Adjust the salt depending on whether the tomato water was seasoned during preparation.
