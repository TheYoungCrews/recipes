# Nonino Aperol Gin Sour

DESCRIPTION
A silky, bittersweet citrus sour with gin, Amaro Nonino, Aperol, and aquafaba.

INGREDIENTS
- 1½ oz Jam Jam Gin
- ½ oz Amaro Nonino
- ¾ oz Aperol
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¾ oz aquafaba
- Optional: ¼ oz simple syrup or honey syrup

INSTRUCTIONS
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker without ice.
2. Dry shake vigorously for 15–20 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake for another 12–15 seconds.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
5. Let the foam settle briefly before garnishing.

GARNISH
- Lemon twist, or a few drops of bitters on the foam (amount not given)
