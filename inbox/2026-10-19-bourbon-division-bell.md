# Bourbon Division Bell

**Category:** Cocktails
**Servings:** 1 cocktail
**Prep Time:** 5 minutes

### Description
A bourbon variation of the classic mezcal-based Division Bell cocktail. This bright, bittersweet drink combines Old Fitzgerald bourbon with Aperol, fresh lime juice, and maraschino liqueur for a balanced cocktail with subtle cherry, floral, and citrus notes.

### Ingredients
- 1½ oz Old Fitzgerald bourbon
- ¾ oz Aperol
- ¾ oz freshly squeezed lime juice
- ½ oz maraschino liqueur
- Ice (amount not given)
- Grapefruit or orange twist, for garnish (optional) (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Add bourbon, Aperol, lime juice, and maraschino liqueur to a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill with ice.
3. Shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds until well chilled.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe glass.
5. Express a grapefruit or orange twist over the drink and garnish, if desired.

### Notes
- This is a bourbon-based variation of the original Division Bell, which traditionally uses mezcal.
- Maraschino liqueur adds delicate cherry and floral complexity rather than pronounced cherry sweetness.
- Fresh lime juice is essential for balancing sweetness and bitterness.
- Old Fitzgerald's wheated bourbon character provides a smooth, rounded base.

**Flavor Profile:** Bright, citrusy, bittersweet, subtly floral, cherry-accented.
