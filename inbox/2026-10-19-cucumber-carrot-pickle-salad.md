# Cucumber Carrot Pickle Salad

added_by: anna
via: chatgpt
source_type: ai
type: side
serves: approximately 4
time: 20–35 minutes including marinating
source: Anna and ChatGPT cooking conversation, 2026-09-29

A tangy, lightly sweet, sesame-scented quick pickle to serve with gochujang beef bowls or other rich rice bowls. This salad intentionally contains NO gochujang.

## Ingredients
- 1 large English cucumber, thinly sliced
- 1–2 carrots, peeled into ribbons or thinly sliced
- ⅓ cup rice vinegar
- 1½ tbsp sugar
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1–2 tsp sesame seeds

## Steps
1. Thinly slice the cucumber and carrot (thin rounds, julienne, or ribbons all work).
2. Whisk rice vinegar, sugar, and kosher salt in a bowl until dissolved.
3. Add cucumber and carrot; toss thoroughly with the dressing.
4. Add sesame oil and sesame seeds; toss again.
5. Refrigerate for 15–30 minutes before serving; 10 minutes will work if rushed, but 20–30 gives a better quick-pickle flavor.
6. Toss again before serving.
