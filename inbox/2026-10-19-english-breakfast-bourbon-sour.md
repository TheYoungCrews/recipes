# English Breakfast Bourbon Sour

**Yield:** 1 cocktail
**Profile:** Bright, malty, silky, lightly spiced

### Ingredients
- 2 oz bourbon (Old Fitzgerald recommended)
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ½–¾ oz homemade English breakfast tea simple syrup
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- Optional: 1 egg white or ¾ oz pasteurized egg white for foam
- Lemon twist for garnish (amount not given)

### Instructions
1. Combine bourbon, lemon juice, tea syrup, and bitters in a cocktail shaker.
2. If using egg white, add it and dry-shake without ice for 15 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake vigorously until thoroughly chilled.
4. Double-strain into a chilled coupe.
5. Garnish with a lemon twist.

### Recipe Notes
- Begin with ½ oz tea syrup and increase to ¾ oz depending on desired sweetness.
- The tannins in English breakfast tea add depth and structure to a classic bourbon sour.
- Egg white is optional but creates a silky texture and beautiful foam.
