# Turkey Kale Farro Soup

**Source/Creator:** Original recipe developed with ChatGPT  
**added_by:** anna  
**via:** chatgpt  
**Serves:** 6–8  
**Prep Time:** 15 minutes  
**Cook Time:** 45–50 minutes  
**Total Time:** Approximately 60–65 minutes

## Ingredients

### Turkey & Vegetables
- 1.5 lbs ground turkey (preferably 93% lean)
- 1 tbsp olive oil, plus more as needed
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 2–3 medium carrots, peeled and diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced

### Seasonings
- 1½ tsp salt, plus more to taste
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
- ½ tsp fennel seeds (optional, for Italian sausage-like flavor)
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce or soy sauce

### Broth, Farro & Cream
- ¾ cup uncooked farro, rinsed
- 7 cups low-sodium chicken broth, plus more as needed
- 1 cup milk or half-and-half
- 2 tsp cornstarch or flour (optional, for gentle thickening)

### Finishing Ingredients
- 1 small bunch kale (lacinato or curly), tough stems removed, leaves torn into bite-sized pieces
- ½ cup grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano
- Juice of ½ lemon

## Instructions

1. **Brown the turkey.** Heat olive oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat. Add ground turkey, season lightly with salt and pepper, and cook for 6–8 minutes, breaking into crumbles until browned and cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
2. **Sauté the vegetables.** In the same pot, add onion, carrots, and celery. Sauté for 5–7 minutes until softened and lightly golden. Add garlic and cook for another 30 seconds.
3. **Build the flavor.** Stir in tomato paste, Italian seasoning, crushed red pepper flakes, and fennel seeds. Cook for 1–2 minutes, stirring frequently, to deepen the flavors.
4. **Add farro and simmer.** Return turkey to the pot. Add Worcestershire sauce, chicken broth, and rinsed farro. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to maintain a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for approximately 25–30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until farro is tender but pleasantly chewy. Adjust cooking time according to the farro variety.
5. **Add kale and creaminess.** If using cornstarch, whisk it into the cold milk or half-and-half. Pour into the soup and stir in the kale. Simmer gently for another 5–7 minutes until the kale is tender and the broth is lightly creamy.
6. **Finish the soup.** Reduce heat to low. Gradually stir in Parmesan or Pecorino Romano, followed by lemon juice. Taste and adjust salt, pepper, and acidity as needed.
7. **Serve.** Ladle into bowls and finish with additional freshly grated Parmesan and cracked black pepper, if desired.

## Notes

- **Kale preparation:** No need to massage lacinato kale before cooking. Curly kale can be gently massaged for 20–30 seconds if desired, but simmering will naturally tenderize it.
- **Farro:** Pearled farro generally cooks faster than whole farro. Follow package timing and add additional broth if necessary.
- **Consistency:** Farro absorbs liquid as it cooks. Add another ½–1 cup broth or water if the soup becomes too thick.
- **Heat management:** Maintain a gentle simmer rather than a vigorous boil to prevent excessive broth evaporation.
- **Extra richness:** Stir in 1 tbsp butter with the Parmesan for a silkier finish.
- **Storage:** Refrigerate leftovers promptly in airtight containers for up to 3–4 days. Farro continues absorbing broth, so thin with additional broth or water when reheating.
- **Flavor profile:** Savory Italian herbs, tender vegetables, nutty farro, earthy kale, and a lightly creamy Parmesan broth balanced with fresh lemon.
