# Gochujang Beef Bowls

A savory-sweet gochujang beef bowl with onion and bell pepper, served over rice with a bright, crunchy quick-pickled cucumber and carrot salad.

Ingredients

For the beef:
1 lb shaved steak or thinly sliced beef
1 medium onion, thinly sliced
1 bell pepper, thinly sliced
1 Tbsp gochujang
3 Tbsp soy sauce
1 Tbsp brown sugar
2 tsp toasted sesame oil
2 tsp grated fresh ginger
2 tsp grated fresh garlic
1 Tbsp water
Neutral oil, for cooking if needed
Green onions, sliced, for garnish
Sesame seeds, for garnish
Cooked rice, for serving

For the quick-pickled cucumber and carrot salad:
1 large English cucumber, thinly sliced
1–2 carrots, julienned, thinly sliced, or shaved into ribbons
1/3 cup rice vinegar
1 1/2 Tbsp sugar
3/4 tsp kosher salt
1 tsp toasted sesame oil
1–2 tsp sesame seeds
1 scallion, thinly sliced, optional
1–2 tsp fresh lime juice, optional

Instructions

1. Make the marinade.
In a large bowl or container, stir together the gochujang, soy sauce, brown sugar, sesame oil, ginger, garlic, and water.

2. Marinate the beef and vegetables.
Add the beef, onion, and bell pepper and gently toss to coat.

If using very thin shaved steak, marinate for up to 4 hours.

If using slightly thicker sliced beef that will hold its shape better, marinate for up to 24 hours.

3. Make the quick-pickled cucumber and carrot salad.
Whisk together the rice vinegar, sugar, and salt until mostly dissolved. Add the cucumber and carrot and toss well. Stir in the sesame oil, sesame seeds, scallion, and lime juice if using.

Refrigerate for at least 15–30 minutes before serving.

4. Cook the beef mixture.
Heat a large skillet over medium-high to high heat.

Add the marinated beef, onion, and bell pepper to the hot skillet. If the pan is crowded, cook in batches so the beef can sear rather than steam.

Let the beef sit briefly against the hot pan before moving it, then gently turn and toss with tongs or a broad spatula until the beef is cooked through and the onions and peppers are tender.

5. Assemble the bowls.
Spoon cooked rice into bowls and top with the gochujang beef, onions, and peppers.

Garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds.

Serve with the quick-pickled cucumber and carrot salad alongside or on top.
