Fresh field peas (pink-eyes, purple hulls) simmered creamy with smoked ham hock, onion and bacon drippings. Southern Living Test Kitchen.

## Original
Makes 4 cups.
- 2 smoked ham hocks
- 1 1/2 cups finely chopped onion
- 3 tablespoons bacon drippings
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 cups shelled fresh field peas (about 1 lb.)
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon ground black pepper

1. Bring hocks and 2 qt. water to a boil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat; simmer 1 1/2 to 2 hours or until meat is tender.
2. Meanwhile, saute onion in hot drippings in a skillet over medium-high 6 minutes. Add garlic; saute 1 minute. Add peas and onion mixture to the Dutch oven with the hocks; bring to a simmer over medium. Cover and simmer, stirring occasionally, 45 minutes or until peas are tender. Remove hocks; drain peas, and sprinkle with salt and pepper.
3. Remove and chop ham from hock bones; discard bones. Stir ham into peas, if desired.
