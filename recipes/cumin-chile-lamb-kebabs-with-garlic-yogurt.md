Sichuan-pepper, cumin and caraway crusted lamb skewers, charred hot and served with lemony garlic yogurt. From White Oak Pastures (Bluffton, GA).

## Original
Garlic yogurt
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- 1/2 tsp finely grated lemon zest
- 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
- Kosher salt, freshly ground pepper

Lamb and assembly
- Cumin seeds (amount not given; used in the spice blend step but missing from the ingredient list)
- 2 tbsp Sichuan peppercorns or 1 tsp black peppercorns
- 2 tsp caraway seeds
- 2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
- 1 tsp sugar
- Vegetable oil (for grill)
- 1.25 lbs boneless lamb shoulder, cut into 1-inch pieces (or lamb kabob meat)
- Kosher salt, freshly cracked pepper
- Finely grated lemon zest (for serving)
- Special equipment: spice mill or mortar and pestle; eight 8-12 in. metal skewers

1. Stir garlic, lemon zest and lemon juice into yogurt; season with salt and pepper. (Can be made 3 days ahead, covered and chilled.)
2. Coarsely grind cumin seeds, peppercorns, caraway, red pepper flakes and sugar until only a few whole spices remain.
3. Prepare a grill for medium-high, indirect heat. Oil grates. Thread lamb onto 6-8 skewers with small gaps. Season with salt, then sprinkle generously with spice blend, pressing it on.
4. Grill over direct heat, turning every minute or so, until browned and charred in spots, about 4 minutes. Move to the cooler side and grill about 4 minutes more for medium-rare.
5. Top yogurt with cracked pepper and a little lemon zest. Serve with the lamb. (Spice blend keeps 1 month airtight.)
