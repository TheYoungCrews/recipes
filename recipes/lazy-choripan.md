## Original

## Our version
White Oak Pastures pork bratwurst, beer-braised in the Dutch oven, butterflied and seared hard, then served open-face on toasted sourdough. A lazy nod to Argentine choripán, without chimichurri. Use the Dutch oven for the brats and the nitrided steel 10-inch pan for the toast; a small cast iron is too tight for this.

- [ ] bratwurst links
- [x] butter (a pat, plus more for the toast)
- [x] onion (sliced)
- [ ] cremini mushrooms
- [x] salt
- [ ] thyme (a pinch)
- [ ] Oktoberfest beer (about half a bottle or can)
- [x] sourdough
- [ ] red wine vinegar (a splash)
- [x] red pepper flakes (a pinch)
- [ ] parsley (torn)

**Steps**
1. Put the Dutch oven over medium heat with the butter (a pat). Brown the brats all over, about 5 minutes, then pull them out.
2. Add the onion (sliced), the cremini mushrooms, salt and the thyme (a pinch). Cook, scraping up the browned bits, until soft and golden, about 12 minutes.
3. Pour in the Oktoberfest beer (about half a bottle or can) and nestle the brats back in. Cover and simmer on medium-low until they hit 160°F: 12 to 15 minutes for raw links, or 6 to 8 if they're fully cooked.
4. Pull the brats and split them lengthwise without cutting all the way through, so they open flat. Uncover the pot, turn up the heat and cook the beer down until the onions are jammy. Scoop the onions out.
5. Put the brats back in the hot pot cut side down and sear them hard, 2 to 3 minutes a side.
6. Butter the sourdough (more for the toast) and toast it in the nitrided steel 10-inch pan.
7. Build it: toast, then onions and mushrooms, then the split brat. Finish with the red wine vinegar (a splash), the red pepper flakes (a pinch) and the parsley (torn).
