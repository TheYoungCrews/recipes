Katie Button's (@chefkatiebutton) boneless skin-on chicken thighs simmered in red wine, rosemary and crushed tomatoes, from Spanish Accent.

## Original

**Boneless Chicken Thighs with Red Wine & Rosemary**, Katie Button, from Spanish Accent: American Home Recipes with a Spanish Touch (Knopf, 2026). Reprinted in WNC Magazine (https://wncmagazine.com/feature/spanglish_home_cooking). Serves 4. No prep or cook time given.

- 2 lbs. boneless, skin-on chicken thighs
- 2 tsp. Diamond Crystal kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp. freshly ground black pepper
- 2 Tbs. extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 large yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1 large carrot, finely chopped
- 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 3 sprigs rosemary
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 1 (14-oz.) can crushed tomatoes

1. Season the chicken with the salt and black pepper on all sides. In a large, wide pan, like a braiser or rondeau, heat the olive oil over medium-high heat. Working in batches, if necessary, to avoid overcrowding, add the chicken and sear on both sides until nicely browned, one to two minutes per side. Transfer the chicken to a plate.
2. Add the onion, carrot, celery, garlic, and rosemary to the pan, and cook until tender and beginning to brown, about five minutes.
3. Add the red wine, stirring to dissolve any browned bits on the bottom of the pan. Bring the wine to a simmer over medium-high heat and nestle the chicken in the pan, skin-side up. Cook until the wine is reduced by half, about three minutes.
4. Add the crushed tomatoes, stirring gently. Reduce the heat to medium and bring to a simmer. Cover and cook for eight minutes. Uncover and simmer until the chicken is cooked through, another eight minutes, gently stirring now and then so the sauce doesn't stick to the bottom of the pan. Discard the rosemary sprigs and serve hot.

## Our version

Same method and amounts as the original. A braiser, the nitrided steel pan, or the Le Creuset Dutch oven all work in place of the wide pan the recipe calls for. No total time was given in the source. The sear is 1 to 2 minutes per side, then about 5 minutes for the vegetables, 3 minutes to reduce the wine, 8 minutes covered, and 8 minutes uncovered.

If boneless skin-on chicken thighs are hard to find, bone-in thighs work with roughly 10 more minutes of covered simmer (cook to 175°F), or use skinless boneless thighs.
