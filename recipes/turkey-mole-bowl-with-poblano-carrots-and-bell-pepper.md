Sheet-pan roasted poblano, carrots and bell pepper with skillet turkey-mole and black beans, topped with pickled onions. Historical recovery; finished batch amounts unverified.

## Original
Suggested starting quantities from the original discussion (not verified in the finished batch):
- 1 poblano pepper
- Approximately 6 oz ground turkey
- Approximately 2 tbsp mole sauce
- 1–2 tbsp black beans
- Half to one bell pepper, sliced
- 1–2 carrots, peeled and thinly sliced
- Paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper (amount not given)
- Lemon juice (amount not given)
- Pickled red onions (amount not given)
- Olive oil or oil spray (amount not given)
- Optional hot sauce (amount not given)

**Suggested method**
1. Cut the bell pepper and carrots into thin strips or coins. Toss with oil, salt and pepper. Add halved, deseeded poblano to the tray.
2. Roast at 400°F for about 15–18 minutes, flipping if needed.
3. Brown ground turkey in a skillet with garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper.
4. Add black beans and mole sauce near the end, then a squeeze of lemon.
5. Serve with the roasted vegetables and poblano, topped with pickled onions and optional hot sauce.
