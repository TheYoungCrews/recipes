A lemony, garlicky, Southern-meets-Mediterranean casserole with prosciutto-wrapped chicken thighs, fresh pink-eyed peas, roasted red pepper almond pesto, arugula, and a Chardonnay pan sauce.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1½–2 cups fresh shelled pink-eyed peas
- Water or chicken broth, for simmering (amount not given)
- 1 garlic clove, smashed
- Salt (amount not given)
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- 8 slices prosciutto
- 4 tablespoons Trader Joe’s Red Pepper Almond Pesto, about 1 tablespoon per thigh
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Freshly cracked black pepper (amount not given)
- Small pinch of salt (amount not given)
- ½ cup Chardonnay
- ½ cup chicken broth
- ½ cup reserved pink-eyed pea cooking liquid
- 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
- Zest of 1 lemon
- Juice of ½ lemon, plus more to finish
- ¼–½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste (amount not given)
- 2 generous handfuls fresh arugula
- Freshly grated Parmesan (amount not given)
- Chopped parsley, optional (amount not given)
- Crusty bread, for serving (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Cook the pink-eyed peas. Rinse the peas and add them to a medium saucepan. Cover with water or chicken broth by 1–2 inches. Add the smashed garlic clove and a generous pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Cook for about 20–40 minutes, or longer if needed, until tender but still holding their shape. Before draining, reserve at least ½ cup of the cooking liquid. Drain and set aside.
2. Make the Chardonnay sauce. Heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the minced garlic and cook for 30–60 seconds, until fragrant but not browned. Pour in the Chardonnay and simmer uncovered for 3–5 minutes, until reduced by about half. In a separate bowl, whisk together the chicken broth, reserved pea cooking liquid, and cornstarch. Whisk this into the reduced Chardonnay and garlic. Add the lemon zest, red pepper flakes, and freshly cracked black pepper. Do not worry about fully thickening the sauce on the stove; it will continue to thicken in the oven. Season lightly, remembering that the prosciutto, pesto, broth, and Parmesan will add salt.
3. Assemble the casserole. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Add the cooked pink-eyed peas to an 8x8- or 9x9-inch baking dish. Pour the Chardonnay sauce over the peas and stir gently.
4. Prepare the chicken. Pat the chicken thighs dry. Season very lightly with salt and generously with black pepper. Spread about 1 tablespoon of red pepper almond pesto over the top of each thigh. Wrap each thigh with 2 slices of prosciutto, keeping the pesto-covered side facing up. Nestle the chicken into the peas and sauce. Drizzle the tops with a little olive oil. If desired, stir an additional teaspoon or two of pesto into the pea mixture.
5. Bake. Cover the baking dish tightly with foil and bake for 20 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 15–25 minutes, until the sauce is bubbling and the chicken reaches at least 165°F internally. Chicken thighs are especially tender around 175–185°F. If the chicken is cooked but the prosciutto needs more color, broil for 1–2 minutes, watching closely.
6. Add the arugula. Remove the chicken thighs from the dish temporarily. Stir the arugula into the hot peas and sauce until just wilted, about 1–2 minutes. Return the chicken to the dish.
7. Finish and serve. Squeeze the juice of about ½ lemon over the finished casserole, then taste and add more lemon if desired. Finish with freshly grated Parmesan, chopped parsley if using, black pepper, and an optional drizzle of olive oil. Serve immediately with warm crusty bread.
