Chuck roast braised with caramelized sweet onions, white wine, and bone broth in a Dutch oven, from Anna Mondanaro (@feelwellbyanna). (Caption and comments; video not fully watched.)

## Original

**Ingredients**
- one chuck roast (3-5 lbs, cut into pieces so it can fit into your Dutch oven)
- one tbsp beef tallow
- 1/2 cup flour
- one tbsp butter
- four to five sweet onions
- three cloves of garlic
- sea salt to taste
- freshly cracked pepper
- two cups beef bone broth
- one cup dry white wine (or sub more bone broth)
- one tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- one tbsp Dijon mustard

**Steps** (from the caption; thyme is named in the steps but is not in the ingredient list, and no amount is given)
1. Preheat oven to 325°F.
2. Start by generously salting chuck roast on all sides and dusting with flour. Cut it up into smaller pieces so they braise more evenly.
3. Heat a Dutch oven over medium heat and sear off the chuck roast. Once it's browned on all sides, remove it from the pan and set aside.
4. Add butter and onions to the Dutch oven. Saute until they're caramelized (about 40 minutes, stirring every five or so minutes).
5. Deglaze the pan with wine and bone broth. Add Worcestershire and Dijon.
6. Add chuck back into the pot, add more salt, thyme, and pepper.
7. Add the lid and braise in the oven for 3-4 hours or until the meat is falling apart.
8. Serve with mashed potatoes or some crusty bread.
