Hong Kong-style pan-seared barramundi with garlic-ginger baby bok choy and a quick soy-vinegar pan sauce. Cooked repeatedly; this is the September 2025 two-fillet version.

## Original
September 2025 version (its sauce is not combined with the January marinade).
- 2 barramundi fillets, or comparable firm white-fish fillets
- 2-3 baby bok choy, halved lengthwise and thoroughly washed
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1-inch piece of ginger, julienned or grated
- 2 tbsp soy sauce or liquid aminos
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar, or lemon juice
- 1 tsp sesame oil, to finish
- Neutral cooking oil, as needed
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional chili flakes or chili crisp
- Optional lemon wedge

1. Pat the fish dry and season lightly with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a thin layer of neutral oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear fish skin-side down if skin-on, about 4 minutes. Flip and cook another 2-3 minutes until cooked through (145°F). Transfer to a plate.
3. Add a little more oil if needed. Sauté garlic and ginger for about 30 seconds.
4. Add the bok choy cut-side down. Sear for about 2 minutes. If the stems are still firm, add a small splash of water, cover briefly and steam until tender-crisp.
5. Add soy sauce and rice vinegar. Let the sauce bubble and coat the bok choy.
6. Finish with sesame oil. Serve the fish with the bok choy and spoon the pan sauce over both. Add chili crisp or lemon if desired.

**January 2025 alternate (marinade for two fillets, not extra sauce):** 3 tbsp liquid aminos, 1 tbsp sesame oil, 2 minced garlic cloves, 1 tbsp grated ginger, 1 tsp rice vinegar or lime, optional 1 tsp honey, 1/4 tsp pepper. Marinate briefly in the fridge, reserve a clean portion of sauce before adding raw fish, and don't use raw-fish marinade as a finishing sauce unless it's boiled.
