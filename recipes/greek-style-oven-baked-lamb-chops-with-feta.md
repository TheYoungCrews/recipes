- Greek salad ingredients: lettuce, tomatoes, cucumbers, onions, olives (amounts not given)

1. Marinate the lamb chops: in a bowl, mix olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, dried oregano, salt, and pepper. Place the chops in a shallow dish and pour the marinade over them. Add fresh rosemary sprigs. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour, or overnight for best results.
2. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
3. Lightly oil a baking dish or Dutch oven. Arrange the marinated chops in a single layer.
4. Crumble feta over the chops, as much or as little as you like.
5. Bake about 25-30 minutes, or until cooked to your desired doneness. Time varies with chop thickness and your oven.
6. Rest a few minutes before serving so the juices redistribute. Serve with any pan juices and more fresh rosemary if desired.
7. Serve with Greek salad.
