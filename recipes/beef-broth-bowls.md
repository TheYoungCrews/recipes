## Original
**Ingredients**
- 1 cup basmati rice
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 1 pound lean ground beef (93/7)
- 1-1/2 tablespoons finely minced ginger, divided
- 1-1/2 tablespoons finely minced garlic, divided
- 2 teaspoons cornstarch
- 1/4 cup chicken broth
- 2-1/2 tablespoons soy sauce (regular, not lite)
- 2 teaspoons white sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon toasted sesame oil, plus more for topping
- 1/4 teaspoon white pepper
- 2 tablespoons red curry paste
- 1 teaspoon yellow curry powder
- 1 can (13.66 oz) coconut milk
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce (regular, not lite)
- 1 large lime, divided
- 1/4 cup finely chopped cilantro, plus more for topping
- Toasted sesame seeds
- 2 small Persian cucumbers, diced
- 4 green onions, thinly sliced
- Chili crunch (optional)

**Steps**
1. Rice: boil generously salted water, add rice, boil 10 minutes until tender. Drain, return to the dry pot, cover and steam until serving.
2. Beef: heat coconut oil in a large skillet over high heat. Add ground beef and leave undisturbed a few minutes to develop browned spots. Break up and cook, stirring only occasionally, until browned all over.
3. Sauce: shake cornstarch and 1/4 cup chicken broth smooth in a jar. Add soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, and white pepper; shake again.
4. Reduce heat to medium. Add 1 tablespoon each garlic and ginger; cook 30 seconds until fragrant. Pour in the sauce; stir 1-2 minutes until thickened and caramelized onto the beef. Remove to a plate and cover.
5. Broth: same skillet over low heat. Cook remaining 1/2 tablespoon each garlic and ginger 20 seconds. Stir in red curry paste and curry powder; cook 1 minute. Pour in coconut milk, scraping up the browned bits; whisk smooth. Add 1 cup chicken broth; boil 1 minute, then reduce to low. Stir in 1 tablespoon soy sauce, juice of 1/2 lime, and cilantro.
6. Assemble: divide rice among 3-4 bowls, add beef alongside, ladle hot broth around everything. Top with cucumber, sesame seeds, green onions, extra cilantro, and a light drizzle of sesame oil. Add chili crunch for heat if desired. Serve with lime wedges.

**Steps**
Weeknight version (aiming for about 30 minutes, Dutch oven, more lime/herbs/umami, high-protein):
1. Start the rice as in the Original.
2. Heat coconut oil in the Dutch oven over high heat. Brown the beef hard, undisturbed, for caramelized edges, then break up and finish browning.
3. Shake the cornstarch slurry as in the Original, using liquid aminos (standing sub for soy sauce) in place of the soy sauce. Add garlic and ginger, then the sauce, and cook until caramelized onto the beef. Remove the beef.
4. Build the broth in the same pot (Original step 5), using liquid aminos for the finishing soy sauce.
5. Assemble bowls and finish each with extra lime juice, double the cilantro, and sambal oelek for heat.
