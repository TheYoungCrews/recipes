Lamb and venison meatballs seared, then simmered in a red-wine tomato sauce. Two-meat combo and sear-then-simmer method chosen while cooking in December 2024.

## Original
For the meatballs:
- 1 lb ground lamb
- 1 lb ground venison or a ground venison blend
- 1/2 cup breadcrumbs
- 1/4 cup finely grated Parmesan or Pecorino
- 1 large egg
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Optional 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- About 1 tsp salt, adjusting to taste
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- Olive oil, for searing

For the sauce:
- 1 can crushed tomatoes, approximately 14 oz, or a similar amount of prepared red pasta sauce
- 1/2 cup red wine
- 1 tsp oregano or Italian seasoning
- Optional crushed red pepper
- Extra grated cheese or basil, optional, for serving

1. Gently combine the meatball ingredients. Form meatballs about 1-1 1/2 inches in diameter.
2. Heat a little olive oil in a wide skillet. Sear the meatballs in batches until browned outside, about 4-5 minutes per batch. They don't need to be cooked through. Remove from the pan.
3. Add the red wine and scrape up the browned bits. Simmer briefly to reduce.
4. Stir in the tomatoes or red sauce and oregano. Return the meatballs to the pan.
5. Cover loosely and simmer gently until fully cooked, approximately 20-30 minutes depending on size. Ground lamb and venison should reach 160°F.
6. Serve with extra cheese or basil if desired. Pasta, polenta or bread are optional suggestions, not a recorded part of the original meal.

**Keep:** lamb and venison together; brown first; finish simmering in red sauce with red wine. The original venison was a ground ancestral blend; plain venison is an adaptation.
