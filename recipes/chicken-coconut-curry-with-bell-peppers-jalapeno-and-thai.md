Skillet chicken curry in coconut milk with bell pepper, jalapeño and Thai chili paste, finished with green onion. Cooked repeatedly; documented December 2024 version.

## Original
Consolidated from the written recipe plus requested additions (a reconstruction, not an exact recovery). For 1 lb chicken:
- 1 lb boneless chicken breast or thighs, bite-size pieces
- 1 can coconut milk, approximately 14 oz
- 1 bell pepper, sliced (red in the written recipe)
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp freshly grated ginger
- 2 tbsp curry powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 jalapeño, finely chopped
- Thai chili paste, to taste
- Green onions, thinly sliced, to finish
- Salt and black pepper
- Optional chopped cilantro

1. Heat the oil in a wide skillet. Sauté the onion until softened, about 3-4 minutes.
2. Add garlic, ginger and jalapeño. Cook for 1-2 minutes.
3. Add curry powder, cumin, coriander, turmeric and Thai chili paste. Stir briefly until fragrant.
4. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
5. Add coconut milk and bell pepper. Simmer uncovered until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened, approximately 10-15 minutes. Chicken should reach 165°F.
6. Taste for salt, pepper and chili. Finish with green onions and optional cilantro.

**Personal changes:** jalapeño, Thai chili paste and green onions were specifically requested additions. Peppers added after the chicken released too much water; sear them separately and add back near the end. If the pan is watery, simmer uncovered to reduce, pulling cooked chicken out if needed.

**Uncertain:** amounts of chili paste and green onion never settled; breast vs thigh not recorded; quantities are the recipe suggestion, not a measured record. A later version added broth and marinated chicken inconsistently; neither is part of this one.
