Whipped Georgia sweet potatoes under a buttery brown-sugar pecan crumble. The fall and Thanksgiving classic. Southern Living.

## Original
Soufflé
- 4 large or 5 medium sweet potatoes (about 6 cups mashed flesh)
- Cooking spray
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 teaspoon salt

Pecan topping
- 2/3 cup light brown sugar
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1 1/2 cups pecan halves
- 5 tablespoons melted butter

1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Wash and dry sweet potatoes, wrap in foil, and bake on a foil-lined sheet 1 1/2 hours. Cool until the skins come off easily, about 30 minutes.
2. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x13 glass dish. Beat sweet potatoes on medium-high until smooth, about 2 minutes. Add butter, brown sugar, granulated sugar and cinnamon; mix. Add eggs, milk, vanilla and salt; beat until homogenous. Pour into the dish.
3. Stir together brown sugar, flour, salt and cinnamon; toss in pecans; stir in melted butter. Scatter over the top.
4. Bake until the center is set and doesn't jiggle, 45 to 55 minutes.
