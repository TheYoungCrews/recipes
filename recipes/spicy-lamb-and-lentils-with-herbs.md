Crispy smashed ground lamb with cumin and chile, lentils crisped in the lamb fat, piles of herbs and cucumber over Greek yogurt. From White Oak Pastures.

## Original
Serves 4.
- 1/2 lb White Oak Pastures grassfed ground lamb
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
- 2 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
- 1.5 cups cooked brown or French green lentils (from 1 cup dried)
- 1/2 English hothouse cucumber, chopped
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley, plus leaves for serving
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro, plus leaves for serving
- 3/4 cup plain whole milk Greek yogurt
- Flatbread or rice, for serving

1. Heat oil in a medium skillet over medium-high.
2. Season lamb and press it into one even patty about 1/4" thick in the pan. Cook, pressing occasionally, until the underside is brown and very crisp, about 5 minutes.
3. Pour off excess fat and save. Turn the patty and cook until the other side is crisp, about 5 minutes.
4. Turn heat low, break the lamb into small pieces, and mix in garlic, red pepper flakes and cumin. Back to medium-high, cook about 2 minutes until fragrant and cooked through but juicy. Transfer to a bowl with a slotted spoon.
5. Add lentils and 2 tablespoons reserved fat to the skillet; season and cook, tossing occasionally, until lentils brown and crisp, 5-8 minutes.
6. Return lamb, toss to warm, then off heat stir in cucumber, cilantro and parsley.
7. Spoon yogurt onto plates, top with the lamb mixture and more herbs. Serve with lemon wedges and flatbread or rice.

**Steps**
1. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high. Season the lamb and press it into one thin 1/4-inch patty; cook without moving, pressing now and then, until very crisp underneath, about 5 minutes.
2. Pour off and save the fat. Flip and crisp the other side, about 5 minutes.
3. Drop the heat, break the lamb up, stir in the garlic, pepper flakes and cumin, then cook on medium-high about 2 minutes. Lift it out with a slotted spoon.
4. Crisp the lentils in 2 tbsp of the saved fat with salt and pepper, 5-8 minutes.
5. Return the lamb, toss, then off heat fold in the cucumber, cilantro and parsley.
6. Spread yogurt on plates, pile the lamb and lentils on top, add more herbs, and serve with lemon wedges and rice.
