Classic braised beef stew with bacon, pearl onions and sauteed mushrooms, adapted from Julia Child's "Mastering the Art of French Cooking." Creator: Julia Child (food.com submission by Chef Kate).

## Original
Serves: (not given on page)

**For the stew**
- 6 ounces bacon, solid chunk
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 3 lbs lean stewing beef, cut into 2-inch cubes
- 1 carrot, peeled and sliced
- 1 onion, peeled and sliced
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon pepper, freshly ground
- 2 tablespoons flour
- 3 cups red wine (a full bodied wine like Bordeaux, Burgundy or Chianti)
- 2-3 cups beef stock (unsalted and defatted)
- 1 tablespoon tomato paste
- 2 garlic cloves, mashed (you may choose to add more)
- 1 sprig thyme (or 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme)
- 1 bay leaf, preferably fresh

**For the braised onions**
- 18-24 white pearl onions, peeled
- 1 1/2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 cup beef stock
- salt and fresh ground pepper (amount not given)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 sprig thyme
- 2 sprigs parsley

**For the sauteed mushrooms**
- 1 lb mushrooms, quartered
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

**Steps**
1. Cut the rind off the bacon and reserve it. Cut the bacon into lardons about 1/4" thick and 1 1/2" long.
2. Simmer the rind and lardons for 10 minutes in 1 1/2 quarts of water. Drain and dry them and reserve.
3. Preheat the oven to 450F.
4. Warm the tablespoon of olive oil in a large (9"-10" wide, 3" deep) fireproof casserole over moderate heat. Saute the lardons 2 to 3 minutes to brown lightly. Remove to a side dish with a slotted spoon.
5. Dry the beef and saute it a few pieces at a time in the hot oil/bacon fat until nicely browned on all sides. Remove to the side plate with the bacon.
6. In the same fat, saute the onion and carrot until softened.
7. Pour off the fat and return the lardons and beef to the casserole with the carrots and onion. Toss with the salt and pepper and sprinkle with the flour.
8. Set the uncovered casserole in the oven for 4 minutes. Toss, and return to the hot oven for 4 more minutes.
9. Lower the oven to 325F and remove the casserole. Add the wine and enough stock that the meat is barely covered. Add the tomato paste, garlic, herbs and bacon rind.
10. Bring to a simmer on the stovetop. Cover and place in the oven, adjusting the heat so the liquid simmers very slowly for 3 to 4 hours. The meat is done when a fork pierces it easily.
11. Meanwhile, prepare the onions: if using frozen, defrost and drain them. Heat the butter and oil in a large skillet and add the onions. Saute over medium heat about 10 minutes, rolling them so they brown evenly without breaking apart.
12. Pour in the stock, season to taste, add the herbs and cover. Simmer over low heat 40 to 50 minutes until the onions are tender but keep their shape and the liquid has mostly evaporated. Remove the herbs and set the onions aside.
13. For the mushrooms, heat the butter and oil over high heat in a large skillet. As soon as the foam begins to subside, add the mushrooms and toss and shake the pan about 5 minutes, until lightly browned. Remove from heat.
14. To finish: when the meat is tender, empty the casserole into a sieve set over a saucepan. Wash out the casserole and return the beef and bacon to it (discard the bits of carrot, onion and herbs left in the sieve).
15. Distribute the mushrooms and onions over the meat.
16. Skim the fat off the sauce and simmer it a minute or two, skimming any more that rises. You should have about 2 1/2 cups of sauce thick enough to coat a spoon lightly. If too thick, add a few tablespoons of stock. If too thin, boil it down. Taste for seasoning.
17. Pour the sauce over the meat and vegetables. If serving immediately, simmer the covered casserole over medium-low heat 2 to 3 minutes. Serve in the casserole or on a warm platter with noodles, potatoes or rice, garnished with fresh parsley.
18. If serving later or the next day, cool and refrigerate the covered casserole. 20 minutes before serving, simmer very slowly over medium-low heat for 10 minutes, occasionally basting the meat and vegetables with the sauce.
