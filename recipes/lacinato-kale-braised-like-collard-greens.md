Rich, savory kale braised until tender with lamb fat, lamb jus, and apple cider vinegar.

## Original
- 1 large bunch (about 1 lb) Lacinato kale (Tuscan/Dinosaur kale)
- 2–3 Tbsp Rendered Lamb Fat (or the fat from your roast)
- 1/4 cup Lamb Jus (or other concentrated meat broth/stock)
- 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups (16 oz) Chicken or Vegetable Broth (or water)
- 1/4 cup Apple Cider Vinegar (for essential tang)
- 1 teaspoon granulated sugar (to balance bitterness and tang)
- 1 dried Bay Leaf
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional, for heat)
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

**Instructions**
1. Prepare the Kale: Wash the kale thoroughly. Fold the leaves in half lengthwise and slice out the tough center stems (ribs); discard them. Stack the leaves and slice them into roughly 1-inch strips.
2. Sauté Aromatics: In a large Dutch oven or heavy-bottomed pot, melt the lamb fat over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and sauté until soft and translucent, about 5–7 minutes. Add the minced garlic and red pepper flakes (if using) and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add Kale and Jus: Add the sliced kale to the pot. Stir and cook for about 3–5 minutes until the kale begins to wilt and shrink down. Stir in the lamb jus.
4. Braise the Greens: Pour in the broth (16 oz), apple cider vinegar, and sugar. Add the Bay Leaf. Bring the liquid to a gentle simmer.
5. Long and Slow Cook: Reduce the heat to low, cover the pot, and let the kale braise for 45 to 60 minutes, or until the kale is very soft and tender. Check every 15–20 minutes; if the liquid cooks down to a thin layer, add an extra 1/2 cup of water or broth.
6. Finish and Serve: Remove and discard the bay leaf. Season generously with salt and pepper. Taste and adjust; a final splash of vinegar or pinch of sugar as needed. Serve hot with pot liquor over the greens.
