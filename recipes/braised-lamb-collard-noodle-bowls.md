Seared lamb and organ meatballs simmered in a garlicky red sauce, finished with parmesan, basil and a cold spoon of ricotta. Bread or polenta on the side.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- [x] sourdough (for the panade)
- [ ] collard greens (1 large bunch, stems removed, chopped)
- [ ] Thai basil
- [ ] green onions
- [x] egg white spaghetti noodles
- [x] anchovy paste
- [x] tomato paste
- [x] ground beef/organ blend (1 lb)
- [x] ground lamb (1 lb)
- [x] fresh limes
- [x] full-fat cottage cheese
- [x] Fire Kettle turmeric-ginger bone broth

**Steps**
1. **Panade & mix:** soak crustless sourdough in a splash of milk or broth, mash to paste. Combine with lamb, beef/organ blend, minced garlic, white parts of green onion, salt and pepper. Golf-ball meatballs.
2. **Hard sear:** Dutch oven, medium-high. Deep crust on all sides; remove. Keep the fat.
3. **Umami base (pinçage):** tomato paste + anchovy paste into the fat, 2-3 min, stirring, until rust-colored.
4. **Deglaze & braise:** bone broth, scrape the fond. Add collards, return meatballs and juices.
5. **Simmer:** gentle bubble, covered, 15-20 min.
6. **Noodles:** boil egg white spaghetti separately; into wide shallow bowls.
7. **Assemble:** heat off, juice of one lime into the broth. Ladle over noodles, cold cottage cheese in the center, green onion tops and Thai basil.

## Our version
**Grocery & inventory check**
- [ ] ground lamb (1 lb)
- [ ] ground beef/organ blend (1 lb)
- [x] sourdough (2 slices, crusts off)
- [ ] milk (1/4 cup)
- [x] parmesan (1/2 cup grated, plus more to finish)
- [x] garlic (6 cloves: 3 minced, 3 sliced)
- [ ] parsley (1/4 cup chopped)
- [ ] fennel seed (1 tsp, lightly crushed)
- [ ] dried oregano (1 tsp)
- [x] olive oil (2 tbsp, plus more to finish)
- [x] onion (1, diced)
- [x] red pepper flakes (1/2 tsp)
- [x] tomato paste (2 tbsp)
- [x] anchovy paste (1 tsp)
- [ ] dry red wine (1/2 cup, optional)
- [ ] crushed tomatoes (one 28 oz can)
- [ ] fresh basil (a handful, torn)
- [ ] whole-milk ricotta or full-fat cottage cheese (for serving)
- [ ] crusty bread or soft polenta (for serving)
- [x] salt (1 1/2 tsp) and black pepper

**Steps**
1. **Mix:** soak the bread in the milk and mash to a paste. Mix with the lamb, organ blend, parmesan, minced garlic, parsley, fennel, oregano, salt and pepper. Roll golf-ball meatballs.
2. **Sear:** olive oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high. Brown the meatballs well on all sides in batches, then set aside.
3. **Base:** lower to medium. Cook the onion in the fat for 5 minutes, then add the sliced garlic and pepper flakes for 1 minute. Stir in the tomato paste and anchovy paste and cook 2 to 3 minutes until rust-colored.
4. **Braise:** add the wine and scrape up the fond, let it bubble off for a minute, then stir in the crushed tomatoes and about 1/2 cup water. Return the meatballs and juices, cover and simmer gently 15 to 20 minutes, until cooked through and the sauce has thickened a little. Taste for salt.
5. **Serve:** into shallow bowls with parmesan, torn basil, a drizzle of olive oil and a spoon of cold ricotta. Bread or polenta on the side for the sauce.
