Saucy household amatriciana with guanciale or pancetta, onion, garlic, white wine and a full 28-oz can of tomatoes. Cooked December 2024.

## Original
Household chat version (includes onion and garlic; not a claim of strict tradition).
- 12 oz bucatini or another long pasta
- 4 oz guanciale or pancetta, diced
- 1 can peeled tomatoes, 28 oz, crushed by hand or chopped
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 1-2 garlic cloves, minced
- Up to 1 tsp red pepper flakes, optional
- 1/4 cup dry white wine
- 1/2 cup grated Pecorino Romano, for finishing
- About 1 tbsp olive oil, as needed
- Salt and black pepper
- Reserved pasta cooking water, as needed

1. Boil a large pot of salted water. Cook the bucatini until just shy of al dente. Reserve about 1 cup of cooking water before draining.
2. In a wide skillet over medium heat, render the guanciale or pancetta until browned and crisp, approximately 5-7 minutes. Add olive oil only if needed. Lift out the meat, leaving enough fat to cook the aromatics.
3. Soften the onion in the pan. Add garlic and optional chili flakes and cook briefly.
4. Add the white wine and let it reduce for 1-2 minutes before adding the tomatoes.
5. Add the entire 28-oz can of tomatoes. Simmer about 15-20 minutes until thickened. Season to taste.
6. Add the bucatini and toss with the sauce, using pasta water as needed. Add the cooked pork and finish with grated Pecorino.
