A runny pumpkin batter that bakes up into a soft, pudding-like pie, finished with orange zest and cardamom whipped cream. Makes about 3 pies, so halve it for one.

## Original

**Pie**
- 2½ cups sugar
- 1 can (15 oz) pumpkin purée
- 2 eggs
- ½ tsp baking soda
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- ½ tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp vanilla
- Pinch of salt
- 2½ cups milk
- 4 tbsp butter, melted
- Pie crusts, store-bought or graham cracker (enough for about 3 pies)
- 1 orange, for zest
- About 1 tsp powdered sugar, for dusting

1. Heat the oven to 325°F.
2. Mix the sugar, pumpkin, eggs and baking soda.
3. Add the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, vanilla and salt, and mix well.
4. Whisk in the milk and melted butter. The batter will be runny, which is normal because it sets in the oven.
5. Fill the crusts to the edge and bake 55 minutes.
6. Let them cool, dust with sifted powdered sugar, and zest the orange over the top.

**Cardamom whipped cream**
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 tbsp powdered sugar
- 1 tsp cardamom
- Pinch of nutmeg

1. Chill a metal bowl and whisk for 15 minutes.
2. Whisk the sugar and spices together, add the cream, and whip to stiff peaks. Serve on the pie.
