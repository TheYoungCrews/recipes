Filipino-style Boston butt simmered low with soy, white wine, brown sugar and anise. Creator: Zeny Domingo.

## Original
Original printed title: Asado Pork.

- 2-lb. pork roast (Boston butt)
- 3 cups water
- 1/3 cup soy sauce
- 1/3 cup brown sugar
- 2 cups white wine
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 cloves garlic
- Anise to taste (amount not given)

1. Pour ingredients over pork roast and bring to a boil.
2. Turn heat to low and simmer for 2-3 hours or until fork-tender.
3. May be cooked in a slow cooker, if desired, for a longer amount of time.
