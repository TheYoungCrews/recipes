Fast weeknight ramen built on White Oak Pastures' pork (or poultry or beef) ramen broth with ginger, garlic, shiitakes, spinach and jammy eggs.

## Original
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tablespoons olive oil (or animal tallow)
- 4 cloves minced garlic
- 1 tbsp fresh grated ginger
- 4 cups White Oak Pastures ramen broth (pork, poultry or beef)
- 4 ounces shiitake mushrooms, or a little more
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce (Braggs Amino Acids can also be used)
- 1 grated carrot
- 2 tbsp chopped chives
- 3 cups water
- 3 cups baby spinach
- 3 packages ramen noodles (Yaki-Soba suggested; roughly 16 ounces)

1. Put eggs in a saucepan, cover with cold water by 1 inch, bring to a boil and boil 1 full minute. Cover and rest up to 10 minutes. Drain, cool, peel and halve; yolks should be jammy.
2. Heat the oil in a large stockpot over medium; cook ginger and garlic 1-2 minutes until aromatic without burning.
3. Whisk in the water, broth, mushrooms and soy sauce. Bring to a boil, then simmer about 10 minutes until mushrooms soften.
4. Add noodles; Yaki-Soba cook in about 3 minutes, others until soft.
5. Stir in spinach, chives and carrots; once the spinach wilts (about 2 minutes) it's ready.
6. Top with the egg halves and serve.

**Steps**
1. Cover the eggs with cold water by an inch, bring to a boil, boil 1 minute, then cover off heat up to 10 minutes. Cool, peel, halve.
2. In the Dutch oven, cook the garlic and ginger in the oil over medium 1-2 minutes.
3. Add 3 cups water, the broth, the shiitakes and the liquid aminos. Boil, then simmer about 10 minutes.
4. Add the noodles and cook until soft (about 3 minutes for yakisoba).
5. Stir in the spinach, chives and carrot until the spinach wilts, about 2 minutes. Top with the eggs.
