British "amazing meatballs": lamb mince, heart and liver with bacon, port-glazed onion, cumin and herbs, wrapped in caul fat or streaky bacon, browned and finished in the oven. Tom Hunt for The Guardian, adapted from Josh Eggleton.

## Original
Serves 6.
- 50g onion, peeled and diced
- 1 garlic clove, peeled and finely chopped
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 30ml port (or red wine)
- 1 lamb heart, trimmed, washed and finely chopped (about 125g trimmed weight)
- 100g lamb liver, trimmed, washed and finely chopped
- 125g bacon scraps, finely chopped
- 200g lamb mince
- 1 pinch ground coriander
- 1 pinch ground cumin
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp rolled oats
- 1 1/2 tbsp (6g) finely chopped flat-leaf parsley, leaves and stalks
- 3 thyme sprigs, leaves picked
- 1 tsp sea salt
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- Caul fat (rinsed) or streaky bacon rashers, for wrapping

1. Saute the onion and garlic in the oil over low-medium heat for 5 minutes until softened. Add the port, boil and reduce to a syrup; set aside.
2. Mince the heart, liver and bacon by cross-chopping with a large knife (or pulse in a food processor until minced, not pureed). Combine with the mince, onion reduction, coriander, cumin, egg, oats, parsley, thyme, salt and pepper. Divide into six, roll into balls, then wrap and seal in caul fat or flattened streaky bacon.
3. Cook over low heat in an ovenproof frying pan for 10 minutes, turning once halfway, until well browned, then transfer to a 180C (160C fan)/350F oven for 10 minutes. Serve with mash, peas and onion gravy.

**Steps**

2. Pulse the heart, liver and bacon scraps in a food processor until minced, not pureed. Mix with the lamb mince, onion syrup, coriander, cumin, egg, oats, parsley, thyme, salt and pepper.
3. Roll into 6 balls and wrap each in flattened streaky bacon (or caul fat).
4. Brown over low heat in an ovenproof skillet 10 minutes, turning once, then finish in a 350°F oven 10 minutes.
