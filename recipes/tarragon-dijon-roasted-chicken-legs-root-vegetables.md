Pastured chicken legs browned, then simmered low in a lemony white wine, cream, mustard and tarragon sauce with potatoes and carrots. One pot. From White Oak Pastures.

## Original
The page lists amounts and ingredients as two separate columns; paired here in the order given.
- 2 TBL olive oil
- 1 TBL kosher salt
- 1 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 3/4 lb red potatoes
- 3/4 lb gold potatoes
- 1 lb carrots
- 1 onion
- 1 lemon
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 pack White Oak Pastures pastured chicken legs
- 3/4 cup white wine
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 2 TBL all-purpose flour
- 1 TBL coarse grain mustard
- 2 tsp dried tarragon

1. Season chicken legs with salt and pepper, then lightly dredge in flour. Brown in the oil over medium-high heat; remove.
2. Add onions and carrots; saute 6-7 minutes until starting to caramelize. Add garlic until fragrant, about 45 seconds.
3. Deglaze with the wine, reduce slightly, then squeeze in the juice of the lemon. Add cream, Dijon and tarragon; stir.
4. Add potatoes and return chicken. Cover and simmer on low 45 minutes until the chicken pulls from the bone and the potatoes are tender. Adjust salt and pepper.

**Steps**
1. Season the legs with salt and pepper and dust with flour. Brown them in the oil in the Dutch oven over medium-high; set aside.
2. Cook the onion and carrots 6-7 minutes until they start to caramelize. Add the garlic for about 45 seconds.
3. Deglaze with the wine, let it reduce a little, then squeeze in the lemon. Stir in the cream, mustard and tarragon.
4. Add the potatoes, nestle the chicken back in, cover and simmer on low 45 minutes until the meat falls off the bone. Season to taste.
