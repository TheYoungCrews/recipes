Creamy scalloped sweet potatoes with a brown butter sage sauce, Gruyère, and Parmesan, from Molly Thompson (What Molly Made).

## Original

**Scalloped Sweet Potatoes**, Molly Thompson, What Molly Made (https://whatmollymade.com/scalloped-sweet-potatoes/). Serves 12. Prep 25 minutes, cook 1 hour 15 minutes, total 1 hour 40 minutes.

**Ingredients**
- 4 Tablespoons unsalted butter
- 6 sage leaves
- 3 Tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1 cup whole milk
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 3 cloves garlic minced
- 4-5 thyme sprigs
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt plus more to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Pinch of nutmeg optional
- 3 lbs sweet potatoes peeled and cut into 1/8-inch round (3mm on a mandoline), about 5-6 large sweet potatoes
- 1 1/2 cups shredded Gruyère cheese divided
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

**Steps**
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Grease a 9x13-inch baking dish with nonstick spray.
2. Brown the butter: Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Continue cooking, stirring frequently, until the butter begins to turn golden. Add the sage leaves during the last 1-2 minutes and cook until the leaves are crisp and the butter is golden brown and smells nutty. Transfer the sage leaves to a paper towel-lined plate and reserve for serving.
3. Make the cream sauce: Whisk the flour into the browned butter and cook for 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the milk and cream, then add the smashed whole garlic cloves, thyme, salt, pepper, and nutmeg, if using. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently, until the sauce lightly coats the back of a spoon. Remove the garlic and thyme stems or strain the sauce through a fine-mesh strainer into a bowl or measuring cup with a pour spout.
4. Layer the potatoes: Arrange half of the sweet potatoes in the bottom of the prepared baking dish. Top with half of the cream sauce and 3/4 cup of the Gruyère. Repeat with the remaining sweet potatoes and cream sauce. Top with the remaining 3/4 cup Gruyère and the Parmesan.
5. Bake: Cover the dish tightly with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Uncover and bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the potatoes are tender, the edges are bubbling, and the cheese on top is browned. If needed, broil for 1-2 minutes to brown the top, watching closely.
6. Rest and serve: Let rest for 20-30 minutes before topping with the reserved fried sage and serving.

Molly's notes: bagged organic sweet potatoes tend to be a good size for even rounds. If using a mandoline, set it to 3 mm and use the hand guard and a cut-resistant glove. Make ahead: assemble up to 1 day ahead, but leave off the top layer of cheese; cover and refrigerate, let it sit out for 30 minutes, add the cheese, and bake for an extra 10-15 minutes. Or bake it fully up to 2 days ahead, then reheat covered at 350°F for 30-40 minutes, uncovering for the last 10. To travel: bake right before you leave, wrap in foil and towels, and pack the fried sage separately. Leftovers: store covered in the fridge for up to 4 days; reheat at 350°F covered until warm, or reheat individual portions in the microwave.
