Roast-and-shred pastured chicken simmered with green chiles, ancho, cumin and salsa, with a blender tomatillo-cilantro-lime dressing. A White Oak Pastures staff favorite.

## Original
Tacos
- 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 pasture-raised chicken
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 4 oz. can green chiles, drained
- 1 teaspoon ancho chile powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
- Salt (to taste)
- 1 cup favorite salsa
- 1 tablespoon brown sugar
- 1 cup water
- 1 package yellow corn tortillas
- Optional toppings: cilantro, diced onion, sour cream, cotija, pico de gallo, shredded cabbage, sliced jalapenos, guacamole or avocado

Cilantro lime dressing
- 1 packet (1 oz) Hidden Valley Original Ranch seasoning mix (or equivalent)
- 1 cup mayonnaise
- 1 cup whole milk
- 2-3 large tomatillos, husked and halved
- 1 bunch fresh cilantro, stems included
- 1 tsp minced garlic
- 1/4 tsp ground cumin
- Juice of 1 large lime (extra to taste)
- 1 jalapeno (deseed for milder)
- Optional: one 4 oz can diced green chiles

1. Rub the chicken with butter or oil, season with salt and pepper, truss, and roast breast side up at 400°F about 45 minutes, then in 10-minute increments until the thigh reads 165°F. Cool and shred (about 3 to 3 1/2 cups).
2. Heat olive oil in a skillet; cook garlic and green chiles until sizzling, about 1 minute. Add chile powder, cumin, paprika, oregano and salt; cook 1 minute more.
3. Add salsa, brown sugar and water; simmer about 5 minutes.
4. Add the chicken and simmer until coated and no longer soupy. Salt to taste.
5. Warm tortillas, fill, top as desired, and drizzle with dressing.
6. Dressing: blend everything and chill at least 30 minutes.

**Steps**
1. Ahead of time: rub the chicken with oil, salt and pepper, truss, and roast at 400°F about 45 minutes, checking every 10 minutes after until the thigh hits 165°F. Shred it.
2. Dressing: blend the ranch mix, mayo, milk, tomatillos, cilantro, garlic, cumin, lime juice and jalapeno. Chill at least 30 minutes.
3. Sizzle the garlic and chiles in the olive oil for 1 minute, add the spices and a pinch of salt for 1 minute more.
4. Add the salsa, brown sugar and 1 cup water; simmer 5 minutes. Add the chicken and simmer until saucy, not soupy.
5. Warm the tortillas, fill, top with whatever you like, and drizzle with the dressing.
