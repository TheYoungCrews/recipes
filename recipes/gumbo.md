Chicken and andouille gumbo built on a dark roux and the onion, celery and green pepper trinity. From the New Orleans School of Cooking.

## Original
- 1/4 lb. lard or 1/2 cup oil
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 lb. chicken, cut up or deboned
- 1 lb. Andouille sausage
- 2 cups onions, chopped
- 1 cup celery, chopped
- 1 cup green pepper, chopped
- 1 tbsp. garlic, chopped
- 6 cups stock or flavored water
- 1 cup green onions, chopped
- Joe's Stuff seasoning, to taste
- File powder (1/4 to 1/2 tsp per serving, per the sheet's note)
- Crab boil, to taste
- Rice, optional for serving
- French bread, to accompany

The sheet labels the onions, celery and green pepper as the "TRINITY: (Meant three items)".

1. Season and brown the chicken in 1/4 cup of oil, lard or bacon drippings over medium heat in a large pot. Add sausage to the pot and saute with the chicken.
2. In a skillet, make a roux with equal parts oil and flour to the desired color. The oil must be free of food particles to avoid burning. Add onions, celery and green pepper. Add garlic to the mixture and stir continuously. After the vegetables reach the desired tenderness, add them to the pot with the chicken and sausage, continuing to stir frequently.
3. Gradually stir in liquid and bring to a boil. Reduce heat to a simmer and cook for an hour or more. Season the gumbo to your taste with Joe's Stuff seasoning.
4. Approximately 10 minutes before serving, add green onions. Serve gumbo over rice or without rice, accompanied by French bread.

File is a fine green powder of young dried ground sassafras leaves, used in gumbo for flavor and thickening. It may be placed on the table for individuals to add to their gumbo if they wish. Use 1/4 to 1/2 tsp. per serving.

## Our version

- [ ] file powder (1/4 to 1/2 tsp per bowl, at the table)
- [x] rice, for serving (optional)
- [ ] French bread, to serve

**Steps**
1. Season the chicken and brown it in about 1/4 cup of the fat in a large pot (Dutch oven) over medium heat. Add the sausage and saute with the chicken.
2. In a skillet, make a roux with equal parts clean oil and flour, stirring until it reaches the color you want. Add the onion, celery and green pepper, then the garlic, stirring constantly. When the vegetables are tender, scrape it all into the pot with the chicken and sausage, stirring often.
3. Slowly stir in the stock and bring to a boil. Drop to a simmer and cook an hour or more. Season to taste with Joe's Stuff and crab boil.
4. About 10 minutes before serving, stir in the green onion. Serve over rice (or not) with French bread, and put file powder on the table so everyone can add 1/4 to 1/2 tsp to their bowl.
