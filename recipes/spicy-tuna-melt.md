Dish: Spicy Tuna Melt.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1 tin Fishwife Tuna in Spicy Olive Oil
- 4 slices sourdough bread
- 3–4 oz Trader Joe's Gruyère Cheddar, freshly shredded
- 2 tbsp freshly grated Parmesan
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp finely diced red onion or shallot
- 1 tbsp finely chopped dill pickles
- 1 tsp pickle juice
- 1 tsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp spicy olive oil reserved from the tuna tin
- Butter, for the outside of the bread (amount not given)
- Freshly ground black pepper, optional (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Prepare the tuna filling
2. Drain the tuna, reserving 1 teaspoon of its spicy olive oil.
3. Gently break up the tuna in a medium bowl.
4. Add mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, diced red onion, chopped pickles, pickle juice, lemon juice, and reserved spicy olive oil.
5. Stir in 1 tablespoon of freshly grated Parmesan.
6. Add freshly ground black pepper if desired. Mix gently until combined.
7. Assemble the sandwiches
8. Butter one side of each slice of sourdough bread.
9. Place two slices buttered-side down.
10. Divide the tuna mixture evenly between the two slices.
11. Top each sandwich with 1½–2 oz shredded Gruyère Cheddar.
12. Sprinkle the remaining Parmesan evenly over the cheese.
13. Close with the remaining slices of bread, buttered-side up.
14. Grill the sandwiches
15. Heat a large skillet over medium-low heat.
16. Add the sandwiches and cook for 3–4 minutes, until the bottoms are golden brown.
17. Carefully flip and cook for another 3–4 minutes.
18. Cover the skillet during the final 1–2 minutes to help the cheese melt completely.
19. If the bread is browning too quickly, reduce the heat to low.
20. Remove from the skillet, rest for 1 minute, and slice diagonally.
