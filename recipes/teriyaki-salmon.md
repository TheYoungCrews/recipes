Homemade teriyaki sauce, then oven-baked teriyaki-glazed salmon with sesame asparagus. Creator unknown.

## Original
**Teriyaki sauce**
- 1/2 cup liquid aminos (or low-sodium soy sauce)
- 2 tablespoons water
- 1-2 tablespoons honey or maple syrup (adjust to taste)
- 1 teaspoon rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1 teaspoon minced ginger
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch or arrowroot powder (optional, for thickening)

1. In a small saucepan, combine the liquid aminos (or soy sauce), water, honey (or maple syrup), rice vinegar, minced garlic, and minced ginger.
2. Heat over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it starts to simmer.
3. If you prefer a thicker sauce, mix the cornstarch or arrowroot powder with a tablespoon of water to make a slurry. Stir the slurry into the sauce and keep cooking until it thickens slightly.
4. Taste and adjust sweetness or saltiness by adding more honey (or maple syrup) or liquid aminos.
5. Remove from the heat and let cool slightly before using as a marinade or glaze for the salmon.

**Teriyaki glazed salmon with sesame asparagus**
- Salmon fillets (amount not given)
- Garlic (amount not given)
- Ginger (amount not given)
- Sesame oil (amount not given)
- Asparagus (amount not given)
- Sesame seeds (amount not given)
- Green onions (optional, for garnish; amount not given)
- Cooked rice (optional, for serving; amount not given)

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
2. In a small bowl, mix together the teriyaki sauce, soy sauce, honey, minced garlic, minced ginger, and a splash of sesame oil to create the teriyaki marinade.
3. Place the salmon fillets in a shallow dish or resealable plastic bag, and pour the teriyaki marinade over them. Make sure the salmon is evenly coated. Marinate at least 30 minutes in the refrigerator, or up to overnight for more flavor.
4. While the salmon marinates, prepare the sesame asparagus. Trim the tough ends off the asparagus spears and place them on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Drizzle with a little sesame oil and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
5. Place the marinated salmon fillets on a separate baking sheet lined with parchment paper, skin-side down. Spoon any remaining marinade over the salmon.
6. Place both the salmon and asparagus in the preheated oven. Bake about 12-15 minutes, until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork, and the asparagus is tender yet still crisp.
7. While they bake, prepare any additional garnishes or sides, such as cooked rice.
8. Remove from the oven. Serve the salmon hot, garnished with chopped green onions and additional sesame seeds if desired, alongside the sesame asparagus and cooked rice, if using.
