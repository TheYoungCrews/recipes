Rich, slightly spicy, saucy-but-brothy ramen with browned mushrooms, spinach, crispy chicken and soy-marinated jammy eggs, adapted by Anna from two Dishing Out Health recipes (also https://dishingouthealth.com/spicy-ramen-noodles/).

## Original
- 1 Tbsp neutral oil
- 2-3 medium baby bella mushrooms, sliced
- 3-4 oz fresh shiitake mushrooms, tough stems removed, caps sliced
- 1/4-1/2 small yellow onion, finely diced
- 3 green onions, whites and greens separated
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1-2 tsp Trader Joe's Chili Onion Crunch (chili crisp), plus more to finish
- 3 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth, plus more as needed
- 2 Tbsp white miso paste
- 1 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1/2-1 tsp fish sauce, to taste
- 1 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 packs ramen noodles, seasoning packets discarded
- 2 generous handfuls (about 2 oz) fresh baby spinach
- Chicken nuggets, enough for 2 servings, air-fried until crisp
- Toasted sesame seeds, optional
- Lime wedges, optional
- 4 large eggs
- 3 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 Tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 Tbsp water
- 1 tsp honey or brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp grated ginger, optional

1. Jammy eggs: air fryer at 300F, eggs spaced in the baskets, 9 minutes. Ice bath 5 minutes, peel.
2. Quick marinade: whisk soy sauce, rice vinegar, water, honey, sesame oil and optional ginger. Soak the eggs 20-30 minutes in the fridge.
3. Brown the baby bella and shiitake mushrooms in 1 Tbsp oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high, 5-7 minutes total, until golden.
4. Reduce to medium. Cook the onion and green onion whites 2-3 minutes. Add garlic, ginger, sesame oil and chili crisp for 30-60 seconds.
5. Add 1 Tbsp rice vinegar, scrape the pot, pour in the broth, add 1 Tbsp soy sauce and 1/2 tsp fish sauce. Simmer uncovered 10-15 minutes.
6. Reduce to low. Whisk the miso with about 1/4 cup hot broth, return to the pot, do not boil hard. Adjust seasoning.
7. Add the noodles and simmer 3-4 minutes, adding broth or water if needed.
8. Stir in the spinach for the last 30-60 seconds. No corn or edamame.
9. Air fry the chicken nuggets until crisp, slice just before topping.
10. Divide broth, noodles and mushrooms between two bowls. Top with sliced chicken, halved eggs, green onion greens, sesame seeds and extra chili crisp. Optional squeeze of lime.

## Our version
- [ ] 2-3 medium baby bella mushrooms
- [ ] 3-4 oz fresh shiitake mushrooms
- [x] 1 Tbsp neutral oil
- [x] 1/4-1/2 small yellow onion
- [ ] 3 green onions
- [x] 3 garlic cloves
- [ ] 2 tsp fresh ginger
- [ ] 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
- [x] 1-2 tsp chili crisp (Trader Joe's Chili Onion Crunch), plus more to finish
- [ ] 3 1/2 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- [ ] 2 Tbsp white miso paste
- [x] liquid aminos (1 Tbsp for the broth, 3 Tbsp for the egg marinade)
- [x] 1/2-1 tsp fish sauce
- [x] rice vinegar (1 Tbsp for the broth, 2 Tbsp for the egg marinade)
- [ ] 2 packs ramen noodles, seasoning packets discarded
- [ ] 2 oz fresh baby spinach
- [x] frozen chicken nuggets, enough for 2 servings
- [x] 4 large eggs
- [x] 1 tsp honey
- [x] sesame seeds, optional
- [x] lime wedges, optional

1. **Jammy eggs:** Preheat the air fryer to 300F. Air fry the eggs (4 large eggs) for 9 minutes, 2 per basket with space around them. Chill in an ice bath for 5 minutes, then peel.
2. **Marinade:** Whisk liquid aminos (3 Tbsp), rice vinegar (2 Tbsp), water (2 Tbsp), honey (1 tsp), sesame oil (1/2 tsp) and optional ginger. Soak the eggs in the fridge 20-30 minutes while you make the soup.
3. **Mushrooms:** In the Dutch oven heat neutral oil (1 Tbsp) over medium-high. Add the baby bellas and shiitakes and let brown undisturbed 2-3 minutes, 5-7 minutes total.
4. **Aromatics, same pot:** Reduce to medium. Cook the onion and green onion whites 2-3 minutes. Add the garlic (3 cloves, minced), ginger (2 tsp, grated), toasted sesame oil (1 Tbsp) and chili crisp (1-2 tsp). Stir 30-60 seconds so the garlic does not burn.
5. **Broth:** Add rice vinegar (1 Tbsp) and scrape up the browned bits. Pour in the chicken broth (3 1/2 cups), liquid aminos (1 Tbsp) and fish sauce (1/2 tsp to start). Simmer gently, uncovered, 10-15 minutes.
6. **Miso:** Reduce to low. Whisk the white miso (2 Tbsp) with about 1/4 cup hot broth until smooth, return to the pot, and do not boil hard. Taste and adjust.
7. **Noodles:** Add the ramen noodles (2 packs, no seasoning packets) and simmer 3-4 minutes, adding broth or hot water as needed. Keep it saucy with enough broth to slurp.
8. **Spinach:** Stir in the baby spinach (2 generous handfuls) for the last 30-60 seconds.
9. **Chicken:** Air fry the nuggets until crisp. Slice just before serving.
10. **Serve:** Top each bowl with sliced chicken, 2 halved eggs, green onion greens, sesame seeds and extra chili crisp. Lime if you like.
