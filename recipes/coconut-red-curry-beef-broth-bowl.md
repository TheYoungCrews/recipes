Warming coconut-red curry broth over rice with sticky honey-soy beef mince and cool cucumber. Creator: @cook.withcourtney.

## Original
**Ingredients**
- 200g beef mince
- 1 large clove garlic
- 1cm piece ginger
- 1 tsp chilli oil or chilli paste
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tsp honey
- White rice
- Sliced cucumber
- 2 spring onions
- Sesame seeds
- 1 tbsp red curry paste
- 1/2 tin coconut milk
- 200ml chicken stock
- 2 kaffir lime leaves (optional)

**Steps**
1. Fry the beef mince in a hot pan with avocado oil until browned.
2. Add the garlic, chilli, and ginger; fry for a few minutes.
3. Add the honey and dark soy sauce; cook until sticky. Set aside.
4. In a separate pan, fry the red curry paste for a few minutes.
5. Add the coconut milk and chicken stock; stir and simmer 3-4 minutes.
6. Bowl up the white rice and beef mince; pour over the broth. Top with cucumber and spring onions.

## Our version
**Grocery & inventory check** (serves 1; double for two)
- [ ] ground beef (200g, about 7 oz)
- [x] garlic (1 large clove)
- [ ] ginger (1cm piece)
- [x] sambal oelek (1 tsp; standing sub for chilli oil / chilli paste)
- [x] liquid aminos (1 tbsp; standing sub for dark soy sauce)
- [x] honey (2 tsp)
- [x] rice (1 serving, cooked)
- [ ] cucumber (a few slices)
- [x] green onion (2)
- [x] sesame seeds
- [ ] red curry paste (1 tbsp)
- [ ] coconut milk (1/2 can)
- [ ] chicken broth (200ml)
- [ ] kaffir lime leaves (2, optional)
- [x] lime (to finish; our addition)

**Steps**
1. Brown the beef hard in a hot pan with a little oil, leaving it undisturbed at first for crispy edges.
2. Add garlic, ginger and sambal oelek (standing sub for chilli paste); fry a minute or two.
3. Add honey and liquid aminos (standing sub for dark soy sauce) and cook until sticky. Dark soy is sweeter and darker than aminos, so taste and add a touch more honey if it needs it. Set aside.
4. In a second pan, fry the red curry paste for a couple of minutes, then add coconut milk, broth and lime leaves; simmer 3-4 minutes.
5. Bowl up rice and beef, pour the broth over, and top with cucumber, green onion, sesame seeds and a squeeze of lime.
