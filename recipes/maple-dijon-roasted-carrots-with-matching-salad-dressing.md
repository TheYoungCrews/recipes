Carrots roasted in a maple-Dijon dressing, with enough left over to dress a small salad for two. Savory with a little sweetness.

## Original
For the carrots:
- 1 lb carrots, peeled if desired and cut into even sticks or lengthwise halves

For the shared glaze and dressing:
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar, or lemon juice
- 1/4 tsp salt, adjusting to taste
- Freshly cracked black pepper

For serving:
- A small salad for two, such as arugula or spinach
- Optional thinly sliced red onion or shaved fennel

Optional flavor variations:
- 1/2 tsp grated ginger or 1/2 tsp soy sauce
- Lemon version: 1/4 tsp anchovy paste
- Balsamic version: 1/2 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- Combining anchovy and Worcestershire: 1/4 tsp of each

1. Heat the oven to 400°F convection or 425°F conventional.
2. Whisk the oil, Dijon, maple syrup, vinegar or lemon, salt and pepper until emulsified.
3. Use about 2 tbsp of the dressing to coat the carrots. Spread in one uncrowded layer on a baking sheet.
4. Roast approximately 22-28 minutes, turning halfway, until tender with browned edges. Adjust for carrot size.
5. Keep the remaining clean dressing for the salad. Toss the greens just before serving, or plate the warm carrots with the greens and drizzle dressing over both.
