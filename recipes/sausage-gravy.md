Classic old-fashioned sausage (sawmill) gravy from Sam Merritt at Sugar Spun Run: four ingredients, 15 minutes, made for biscuits.

## Original
Prep 5 min, cook 10 min, serves 6.

- 1 lb (455 g) pork breakfast sausage (sage flavored recommended)
- 1/4 cup (31 g) all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 cups (590 ml) whole milk
- 1/8 tsp crushed red pepper, optional
- salt and freshly cracked black pepper, to taste

1. Place sausage in a skillet over medium/high heat.
2. Break up and crumble the sausage as it cooks until completely browned and no pink remains. Don't drain: keep at least 1 Tbsp, up to 2 Tbsp, of grease; drain only if there is much more.
3. Sprinkle flour evenly over the sausage. Stir frequently until absorbed (about 1 minute).
4. Slowly drizzle in the milk, stirring as you pour. Add crushed red pepper if using.
5. Cook, stirring frequently, until thickened to your liking.
6. Season with salt and pepper and serve warm over biscuits.

Tips from the post: break up flour clumps before adding milk. It looks runny at first and thickens as it cooks; don't add more flour, just keep cooking. Whole milk gives the right consistency. Make ahead: refrigerate overnight, reheat in a skillet over medium-low with a splash of milk.

## Our version
- [ ] pork breakfast sausage (1 lb), sage flavored
- [ ] all-purpose flour (1/4 cup)
- [x] whole milk (2 1/2 cups)
- [x] red pepper flakes (1/8 tsp), optional
- [x] salt, to taste
- [x] black pepper, freshly cracked, to taste
- [ ] biscuits, to serve

**Steps**
1. Brown the sausage in a skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into crumbles, until no pink remains. Don't drain: keep 1 to 2 tbsp of the grease and pour off only if there's a lot more.
2. Sprinkle the flour evenly over the sausage and stir about 1 minute until it's absorbed, breaking up any clumps.
3. Slowly drizzle in the milk while stirring. Add the red pepper flakes if using.
4. Keep cooking and stirring until it thickens to your liking. It looks runny at first; give it time instead of adding more flour.
5. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper and serve warm over biscuits.
6. Leftovers keep overnight in the fridge. Reheat in a skillet over medium-low with a splash of milk.
