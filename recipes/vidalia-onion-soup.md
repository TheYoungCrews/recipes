French onion soup made with Georgia's state vegetable, deglazed with brown ale and sherry and broiled under Gruyère toasts. Southern Living.

## Original
- 3 fresh thyme sprigs
- Kitchen string
- 6 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 6 medium Vidalia onions, thinly sliced
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup brown ale
- 1/2 cup dry sherry
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 6 cups beef stock
- 12 (1/2-inch) French baguette slices, lightly toasted
- 2 cups (8 oz.) shredded Gruyère cheese (Swiss may be substituted)

1. Tie thyme into a bundle with string.
2. Heat a Dutch oven over medium 1 minute; melt butter. Add onions, salt and pepper. Cook, stirring often, 45 minutes or until caramel colored.
3. Increase heat to medium-high; add thyme and garlic, cook 1 minute. Add ale and sherry, scraping up browned bits; cook 10 minutes until syrupy. Reduce to medium, sprinkle in flour, cook 3 minutes without burning. Stir in stock, bring to a light boil, reduce to low and cook 15 minutes. Discard thyme.
4. Preheat broiler with rack 5-6 inches from heat. Ladle into 6 broiler-safe bowls about three-fourths full. Top each with 2 toasts and 1/3 cup cheese. Broil 30 seconds to 1 minute until bubbly. Serve immediately.

Serves 6.
