Quick weeknight stir fry: lean ground turkey and crisp green beans in a sweet-spicy soy-ginger sauce. Creator: Kelly Gellner (Eat Yourself Skinny).

## Original

**Ingredients (stir fry)**
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 lb green beans, trimmed and chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1/2 cup onion, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced

**Ingredients (sauce)**
- 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tsp chili garlic sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1/4 tsp pepper
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes

**Steps**
1. In a large skillet, heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium-high heat. Add green beans, season with salt and pepper.
2. Sauté about 6 minutes until lightly charred, tender but still crisp. Transfer to a bowl.
3. Drizzle the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil into the skillet; add chopped onion. Sauté 2 to 3 minutes until translucent. Add garlic; sauté 30 seconds, don't burn.
4. Add ground turkey; brown until crumbled and fully cooked.
5. While the turkey cooks, whisk together soy sauce, chili garlic sauce, honey, sesame oil, ginger, pepper and red pepper flakes.
6. Return green beans to the pan; pour in the sauce and mix to coat. Simmer 2 to 3 minutes until the sauce thickens. Serve over hot rice.

**Notes from the source (not invented)**
- Author used 93% lean ground turkey; leaner works but is drier.
- Leftovers: 4 to 5 days refrigerated, airtight container.
- Prep 10 min / cook 15 min / total 25 min (site also says "under 20 minutes" in the intro).
- Suggested sides: jasmine/white/brown rice, lo mein or rice noodles, cauliflower rice, or lettuce wraps.
- Garnish mentioned in the narrative (not in the numbered card): green onions and sesame seeds.
