Beer braised short ribs with caramelized onions, based on Tieghan Gerard's (@halfbakedharvest) cider braised short ribs, served with Food Network Kitchen's whole baked sweet potatoes.

## Original

**Cider Braised Short Ribs with Caramelized Onions**, Tieghan Gerard, Half Baked Harvest (https://www.halfbakedharvest.com/cider-braised-short-ribs/). Serves 6. Prep 30 minutes, cook 3 hours, total 3 hours 30 minutes.

- 5 pounds bone in beef short ribs
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- kosher salt and black pepper
- 1 tablespoon salted butter
- 4 yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 2 cups apple cider
- 2 cups chicken broth or dry white wine
- 6 carrots, chopped
- 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh thyme
- 1 apple, quartered (optional)
- 2 bay leaves

Oven:
1. Preheat the oven to 325°F.
2. Season the short ribs with salt and pepper, then rub with flour to coat. Heat a large oven-safe Dutch oven over high heat, sear the ribs on both sides. Remove the ribs to a plate, wipe the pot clean of grease.
3. Add the butter and onions and cook 5 minutes, until softened. Add a 1/2 cup apple cider, season with salt and pepper, and continue cooking another 5-8 minutes, until the cider has evaporated and the onions are lightly golden. Add the thyme, then add the short ribs back to the pot. Add the remaining 1 1/2 cups cider, broth or wine, carrots, garlic, and bay leaves. Arrange the apples around the meat. Cover and roast in the oven for 2 1/2 to 3 hours or until the short ribs are tender and falling off the bone.
4. Remove the bay leaves. Serve the ribs over a bed of mashed potatoes. Drizzle with the pan sauce and onions.

Slow cooker:
1. Season the short ribs with salt and pepper, then rub with flour to coat. Heat a large Dutch oven over high heat, sear the ribs on both sides.
2. To the bowl of your crockpot, add the butter and onions, then place the ribs over the onions. Add thyme, apple cider, broth or wine, carrots, garlic, and bay leaves. Cover and cook on low for 7-8 hours or on high for 5-6 hours. During the last 1-2 hours of cooking, add the apples, arranging them around the meat.
3. Remove the bay leaves. Serve the ribs over a bed of mashed potatoes. Drizzle with the pan sauce and onions.

Tieghan notes the braise can be made a day ahead and reheated on the stove over low.

**How to Bake Sweet Potatoes**, Food Network Kitchen, by Layla Khoury-Hanold (https://www.foodnetwork.com/how-to/packages/food-network-essentials/how-to-cook-sweet-potatoes). February 10, 2023. No count given.

1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil. Wash and scrub the sweet potatoes thoroughly with a brush, then pat them dry. Poke the sweet potatoes with a fork 3 to 4 times to ensure that steam can escape during baking.
2. Place the potatoes on the prepared baking sheet. Bake for 30 minutes, then flip them over. Bake for another 30 minutes, or until the skin feels crisp and the inside is soft and cooked through.
3. Serve the potatoes hot, with your favorite toppings (such as butter, salt and pepper).

Food Network's rule of thumb for whole baked sweet potatoes: 45 minutes to 1 hour at 400°F; 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes at 350°F; 1 1/2 hours at 325°F. They are done when a knife slides easily through the center, or when an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part reads 205 to 212°F.

## Our version
Tieghan's 6-serving Dutch oven braise, with Food Network Kitchen's whole baked sweet potatoes instead of mashed potatoes. Out of apple cider and apples and skipping wine, this version is a beer braise: malty beer (Oktoberfest or similar, not an IPA) with apple cider vinegar and maple syrup or honey for the sweet-tart, and beef stock for the liquid (chicken broth works if that's what's open). The ribs can be braised the night before, then reheated on the stove over low for a weeknight. Bake the sweet potatoes in the same 325°F oven for the last 1 1/2 hours, which is Food Network's time at that temperature. If you are reheating the ribs on the stove, use that same 325°F timing, or Food Network's shorter times: 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes at 350°F, or 45 minutes to 1 hour at 400°F. Onions stay thinly sliced so they caramelize. Scaled to 2 1/2 lbs of short ribs (half of Tieghan's 5 lbs), which serves about 3. The braise liquids and flour are halved; the aromatics stay close to full so there is plenty of sauce and onions. The liquid should come about halfway up the ribs, so add a splash more stock if it looks low. No count was given for the potatoes, so bake as many as you want to serve.

- [x] 1 1/2 tbsp flour
- [x] salt
- [x] black pepper
- [x] 1 tbsp salted butter
- [x] 3 to 4 yellow onions, thinly sliced
- [ ] 3/4 cup beer (malty, Oktoberfest or similar, not an IPA)
- [ ] 1 1/2 tbsp apple cider vinegar
- [x] 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey
- [x] 1/2 tsp brown sugar
- [x] 1 cup beef stock
- [x] 6 carrots, chopped
- [x] 6 cloves garlic, smashed
- [x] 2 tbsp fresh thyme, chopped
- [x] 2 bay leaves
- [x] Sweet potatoes

**Steps**
1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Season the short ribs (2 1/2 lbs, bone-in) with salt and black pepper, then rub with the flour (1 1/2 tbsp) to coat. Heat the Dutch oven over high heat and sear the ribs on both sides (one batch is usually enough at this size). Move them to a plate and wipe the pot clean of grease.
2. Add the salted butter (1 tbsp) and the onions (3 to 4, thinly sliced) and cook 5 minutes, until softened. Pour in 1/4 cup of the beer (3/4 cup total) plus the brown sugar (1/2 tsp), season with salt and pepper, and keep cooking 5 to 8 minutes, until evaporated and the onions are lightly golden.
3. Stir in the thyme (2 tbsp, chopped), then return the ribs to the pot. Add the remaining beer (1/2 cup), apple cider vinegar (1 1/2 tbsp), maple syrup or honey (1 tbsp), beef stock (1 cup), carrots (6, chopped), garlic (6 cloves, smashed), and bay leaves (2). Cover and braise in the oven for 2 1/2 to 3 hours (start checking at 2 hours), until the ribs are tender and falling off the bone.
4. For a weeknight: let the pot cool, then refrigerate the ribs in their sauce overnight. The next day, spoon off any hardened fat and reheat the ribs and sauce in the Dutch oven on the stove over low until hot.
5. For the last 1 1/2 hours of the braise, bake the sweet potatoes in the same 325°F oven. Line a baking sheet with foil. Wash and scrub the sweet potatoes, pat them dry, and poke each one 3 to 4 times with a fork. Set them on the sheet and bake 1 1/2 hours, until a knife slides easily through the center. If you are reheating leftover ribs on the stove, bake the potatoes at 325°F for 1 1/2 hours, or use Food Network's shorter times: 1 hour to 1 hour 15 minutes at 350°F, or 45 minutes to 1 hour at 400°F.
6. Pull out the bay leaves (2). Taste the sauce and add a splash more apple cider vinegar if it tastes flat. Split the sweet potatoes open and serve the short ribs with them, with the pan sauce and onions spooned over.
