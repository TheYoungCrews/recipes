Cozy weeknight honey-ginger chicken in a creamy brothy sauce over white rice, made in one pot (sear + slow cook). Creator: @alltypesofbowls (Casey Cheek).

## Original

Ingredients:
- 1½–2 lbs boneless skinless chicken thighs
- ¾ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ¼ tsp ground ginger
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 shallot, thinly sliced
- 6 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 4 cups chicken broth
- ¼ cup soy sauce
- ¼ cup honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- ½ can full-fat coconut milk
- 1 bunch broccolini
- Cooked white rice, for serving
- For serving: scallions, sesame seeds, chili crisp, lime

Steps:
1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder, and ground ginger. Sear in olive oil (creator used a bella MULTI on sear at 380°F) for 3–4 minutes per side until golden. Remove and set aside.
2. Add shallot, garlic, and fresh ginger to the pot and cook until fragrant. Pour in chicken broth, soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, and sesame oil, scraping up the browned bits.
3. Return the chicken to the pot, switch to slow cook, and cook until super tender and easily shreds — (slow-cook time not given).
4. Remove the chicken, shred into large pieces, and return it to the broth.
5. Stir in ½ can coconut milk, add the broccolini, and cook just until tender and bright green. Taste and adjust with more soy, honey, or lime as needed.
6. Serve over cooked white rice with plenty of broth, finished with scallions, sesame seeds, chili crisp, and a big squeeze of lime.

## Our version

- [ ] coconut milk, full-fat (1/2 can)
- [ ] broccolini (1 bunch)

- [x] green onion, sesame seeds, chili crisp, lime (for serving)

**Steps**
1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, garlic powder and ground ginger. Sear in olive oil 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden. Set aside.
2. Cook the shallot, garlic and fresh ginger in the same pot until fragrant. Add the broth, liquid aminos, honey, rice vinegar and sesame oil, scraping up the browned bits.
3. Return the chicken, cover and cook low until it shreds easily. The creator slow-cooked it and didn't give a time.
4. Pull out the chicken, shred into large pieces, and return it to the broth.
5. Stir in the coconut milk, add the broccolini, and cook just until tender and bright green. Adjust with more aminos, honey or lime.
6. Serve over white rice with plenty of broth, topped with green onion, sesame seeds, chili crisp and a squeeze of lime.
