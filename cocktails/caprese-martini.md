Gin and dry vermouth martini shaken with a fresh cherry-tomato purée, finished with olive oil, black pepper and basil. Creator unknown.

## Original
Yields 2.
- 4 oz London Dry Gin
- 2 oz Noilly Prat Dry Vermouth
- 1/2 oz Chamberyzette
- ~8 ripe cherry tomatoes
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp sherry vinegar
- 2 pinches salt
- Garnish: EVOO (3-4 drops per glass), cracked black pepper, 1 fresh basil leaf per glass

1. Blend: purée tomatoes, lemon juice, sherry vinegar, and salt with an immersion blender.
2. Mix: pour the purée into a shaker with the gin, vermouth, Chamberyzette, and plenty of ice.
3. Shake and strain: shake vigorously for 30 seconds. Double-strain into 2 chilled martini glasses.
4. Garnish: top each glass with 3-4 drops of olive oil, cracked black pepper, and a fresh basil leaf.
