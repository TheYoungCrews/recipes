Smoky, blood-red sour for the Halloween party; batchable without the egg white. Example entry, low priority (weeknight dinners come first).

## Original
- 2 oz mezcal
- 1 oz fresh blood orange juice
- 0.75 oz lime juice
- 0.5 oz agave syrup
- 1 egg white (optional)
- Angostura bitters on top

## Our version (using bar.md)
- bar.md not filled in yet; check bottles before buying.

**Method**
1. Dry shake with egg white, then shake hard with ice. Strain over a big cube; dash bitters on the foam.
2. Batch for a crowd (per 10): 20 oz mezcal, 10 oz blood orange, 7.5 oz lime, 5 oz agave, 5 oz water; chill, shake to order or serve over ice.
