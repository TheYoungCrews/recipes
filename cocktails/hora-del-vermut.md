The Catalan vermouth hour at home: Spanish red vermut over ice with an orange slice and olives. Meant as a happy-hour pour with Spanish potato chips and olives, plus anchovies if you want them.

## Original
- 4 oz Timbal Vermut de Reus Sweet Red

Build in a double rocks glass filled with ice. Garnish with orange slice and 2 olives. As in Catalonia, pair with olives and potato chips.

## Our version
- [ ] Spanish sweet red vermouth (4 oz), well chilled
- [ ] orange (1 slice)
- [ ] green olives (2)

**Steps**
1. Fill a double rocks glass with ice and pour in the vermouth.
2. Garnish with the orange slice and 2 olives on a pick.
3. Serve with Spanish potato chips, more olives, and anchovies if you like.
