A Negroni with overproof aged Jamaican rum in place of gin. Simple and funky. Created by Joaquín Simó (2009).

## Original
- 1 oz (30 ml) overproof aged Jamaican rum (pot still is best)
- 1 oz (30 ml) Campari
- 1 oz (30 ml) sweet vermouth
- Garnish: orange wheel half

1. In a rocks glass with a big rock of ice, add the rum, Campari and vermouth and stir until chilled, about 45 seconds.
2. Garnish with the orange wheel half.
