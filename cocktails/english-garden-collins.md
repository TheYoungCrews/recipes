Cocktail: English Garden Collins.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 2 oz London dry gin
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ½ oz homemade English breakfast tea simple syrup
- 2–3 oz chilled soda water
- Rosemary sprig or lemon twist for garnish (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Combine gin, lemon juice, and tea syrup in a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill with ice and shake until well chilled.
3. Strain into a Collins glass filled with fresh ice.
4. Top with 2–3 oz soda water.
5. Stir gently to incorporate.
6. Garnish with rosemary or a lemon twist. - London dry gin brings out the subtle floral and botanical characteristics of the tea. - The soda water keeps the cocktail crisp and refreshing. - For a more aromatic presentation, gently clap the rosemary sprig between your hands before garnishing.
