An Old Fashioned riff on a split base of rye and applejack with a demerara sugar cube. The book likes Rittenhouse Bonded rye and Laird's Bonded applejack; VSOP calvados works if you can't find applejack. Created by Michael McIlroy & Richard Boccato (Milk & Honey, 2006).

## Original
- 1 demerara sugar cube
- 2 dashes orange bitters
- Dash club soda
- 1 oz (30 ml) rye whiskey
- 1 oz (30 ml) applejack
- Garnish: orange zest

1. In an old fashioned glass, douse the sugar cube with the bitters and a tiny dash of club soda. Muddle into a slightly gritty paste.
2. Add the rye and applejack with a big rock of ice and stir until chilled, about 45 seconds.
3. Zest the orange peel over the glass and garnish with it.
