Batched smoky rosemary paloma topped with Ciao! sparkling water for hosting.

## Original
- 12 oz Additive-Free Blanco Tequila (G4 Blanco)
- 4 oz Artisanal Mezcal
- 10 oz Fresh Grapefruit Juice
- 4 oz Fresh Lime Juice
- 3 oz Reduced Rosemary Simple Syrup
- Ciao! Sparkling Water (to top)
- Fresh rosemary sprigs (garnish)

1. Combine blanco tequila, mezcal, fresh grapefruit juice, lime juice, and rosemary syrup in a 1-liter bottle and chill.
2. Pour over ice in a highball glass until three-quarters full.
3. Top with Ciao! sparkling water and garnish with a slapped rosemary sprig.
