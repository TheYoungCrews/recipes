A smoky riff on the classic Paper Plane, with Del Maguey Vida mezcal, Amaro Nonino, Sapelo aperitivo (Creature Comforts, an orange aperitivo similar to Aperol) and fresh lemon, plus 1/4 oz of the spiced cranberry syrup from https://recipes.crews.fyi/r/freezer-door-spiced-cranberry-paper-plane

## Original
Our own recipe, a riff on the classic Paper Plane. See Our version.

## Our version
Spiced cranberry syrup is the one from https://recipes.crews.fyi/r/freezer-door-spiced-cranberry-paper-plane

- [x] 3/4 oz mezcal (Del Maguey Vida)
- [x] 3/4 oz Amaro Nonino
- [x] 3/4 oz Sapelo aperitivo (Creature Comforts)
- [x] 3/4 oz fresh lemon juice
- [x] 1/4 oz spiced cranberry syrup
- [ ] rosemary sprig, for garnish

**Steps**
1. Shake the mezcal (3/4 oz), Amaro Nonino (3/4 oz), Sapelo aperitivo (3/4 oz), lemon juice (3/4 oz) and spiced cranberry syrup (1/4 oz) hard with ice.
2. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
3. Slap a rosemary sprig and use it as the garnish.
