Bottled gin Negroni with an equal Campari-Cynar bitter blend and a 4:1 Punt e Mes to Lustau vermouth split, diluted for the bottle. About 10 pours of 90 ml.

## Original
Yield: 900 ml, approximately 10 servings of 90 ml.
- 250 ml gin (original: Roku)
- 125 ml Campari
- 125 ml Cynar
- 200 ml bitter sweet vermouth (original: Punt e Mes)
- 50 ml sherry-based red vermouth (original: Lustau Vermut Rojo)
- 150 ml cold water
- Freeze-dried orange wheels, for serving

1. Measure and combine the gin, Campari, Cynar, both vermouths and water.
2. Stir until combined and transfer to a clean 1-liter bottle.
3. Chill before serving.
4. Pour about 90 ml per serving and garnish with a freeze-dried orange wheel; serve over a large cube if desired.

**Batch math:** the undiluted cocktail is 750 ml (250 gin, 250 combined bitters, 250 combined vermouths). 150 ml water brings it to 900 ml and leaves 100 ml headroom in a 1-liter bottle. Water is 20% of the undiluted volume (16 2/3% of the finished batch). No additional bitters.

**Ingredient notes:** keep the bitter-forward vermouth (Punt e Mes) and the rounder sherry-based vermouth (Lustau, made with Amontillado and Pedro Ximénez) distinct rather than replacing both with one generic sweet vermouth. Campari and Cynar stay as named; they define the bitter blend.
