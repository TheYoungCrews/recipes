A citrus-free espresso cocktail with Amaro Nonino, coffee, and a silky aquafaba foam.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1½ oz Amaro Nonino
- 1 oz fresh espresso, cooled
- ¼–½ oz simple syrup
- ½ oz aquafaba

**Steps**
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker without ice.
2. Dry shake vigorously for about 20 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake for another 15 seconds.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
5. Let the foam settle briefly before serving.
