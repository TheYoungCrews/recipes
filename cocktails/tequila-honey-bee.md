Reposado tequila and honey syrup shaken with fresh lemon, served in a mezcal-rinsed rocks glass with bitters floated on top. Creator: Nick Korbee (via Liquor.com).

## Original
**Ingredients**
- 1 dash mezcal, to rinse
- 2 ounces reposado tequila
- 1 ounce honey syrup
- 3/4 ounce lemon juice, freshly squeezed
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- Garnish: lemon peel

**Steps**
1. Rinse a rocks glass with the mezcal to coat the inside, discard the excess, and set aside.
2. Add the tequila, honey syrup, and lemon juice to a shaker with ice and shake until well-chilled.
3. Strain into the prepared glass over fresh ice and top with the bitters.
4. Garnish with a lemon peel.
