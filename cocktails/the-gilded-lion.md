Anna's bittersweet shaken rye sour where Amaro Montenegro and a touch of Campari replace simple syrup.

## Original
- 1.5 oz Rittenhouse Rye
- 0.75 oz Amaro Montenegro
- 0.25 oz Campari
- 0.75 oz Fresh Lemon Juice

1. Combine all ingredients in a shaker tin with ice (optional egg white for foam).
2. Hard shake for 12-15 seconds and double-strain into a chilled coupe.
