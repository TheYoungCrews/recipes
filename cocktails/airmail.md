A rum and honey cousin of the French 75, topped with Champagne. Made for celebrations and early in the night. Created by Bacardi pamphlet, around 1930.

## Original
- 1/2 oz (15 ml) fresh lime juice
- 1/2 oz (15 ml) honey syrup
- 1 oz (30 ml) rum of your choice
- 2 oz (60 ml) Champagne
- Garnish: lime twist

1. Shake the lime juice, honey syrup and rum with ice for 8 to 10 seconds.
2. Strain into a chilled Champagne flute, then add the Champagne.
3. Zest the lime twist over the drink and garnish.
