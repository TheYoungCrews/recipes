Cocchi Americano and St-Germain topped with soda and a grapefruit peel. Light, low-proof, built in the glass.

## Original
Yield: 1 drink.
- 2 oz white quinquina-style aperitivo wine (original: Cocchi Americano)
- 3/4 oz elderflower liqueur (original: St-Germain)
- About 3 oz club soda or sparkling water, to taste
- 1 long strip of grapefruit peel
- Ice

1. Fill a wine glass or double-rocks glass with ice.
2. Add the aperitivo wine and elderflower liqueur.
3. Top with soda and stir gently.
4. Express the grapefruit peel over the drink and drop it in.

**Ingredient notes:** Cocchi Americano is a white aperitivo wine with gentian and cinchona bitterness; dry vermouth is not an equivalent. Core ratio is 2 : 3/4 (8:3). Keep as the single-serve recipe; a sparkling-wine batch variation has not been finalized.
