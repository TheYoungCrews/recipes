A floral, bittersweet, botanical cocktail with a silky aquafaba texture and no citrus required.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1½ oz Jam Jam Gin
- ¾ oz Sapelo Aperitivo
- ¼ oz St-Germain
- ½ oz Noilly Prat dry vermouth
- ¾ oz aquafaba
- Orange peel (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker without ice.
2. Dry shake vigorously for about 20 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake for another 15 seconds.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
5. Let the foam settle before garnishing.
