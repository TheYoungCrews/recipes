A bright, botanical, slightly bitter gin sour with Aperol and dry vermouth.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1½ oz Jam Jam Gin
- ¾ oz Aperol
- ½ oz Noilly Prat dry vermouth
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice
- Optional: ¼ oz honey syrup, for a slightly sweeter drink
- Lemon twist (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds.
3. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
