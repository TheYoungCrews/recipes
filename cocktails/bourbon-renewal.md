A bourbon sour with a little creme de cassis for a dark-berry edge. Shaken, poured over fresh ice, with a lemon wheel on top.

## Original
- 2 oz bourbon
- 1 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1/2 oz creme de cassis
- 1/2 oz simple syrup
- 1 dash aromatic bitters
- Garnish: lemon wheel

1. Shake ingredients with ice.
2. Strain over fresh ice.
3. Garnish with a lemon wheel.
