Split-base mezcal and reposado espresso martini with mole and chocolate bitters, orange peel, and a pinch of Maldon. Historical recovery from Crews's cocktail notes.

## Original
- 1 oz mezcal
- 1/2 oz reposado tequila
- 1 1/2 oz fresh espresso or cold-brew concentrate
- 1/2 oz agave syrup (maple syrup offered as an alternative)
- 1 dash mole bitters
- 1 dash chocolate bitters
- Orange peel
- Tiny pinch of Maldon salt

**Steps**
1. Combine the spirits, coffee, sweetener, and bitters in a shaker with ice.
2. Shake hard for 12–15 seconds.
3. Fine-strain into a chilled coupe or Nick & Nora.
4. Express the orange peel over the drink, then discard it or use it to scent the rim.
5. Finish with a tiny pinch of Maldon salt.
