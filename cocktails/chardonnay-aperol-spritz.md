A light, citrusy dinner cocktail somewhere between an Aperol Spritz and a French 75, using Chardonnay instead of sparkling wine.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1 oz Jam Jam Gin
- 1 oz Chardonnay
- ½ oz Aperol
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice
- 2 oz sparkling water
- Optional: ¼ oz honey syrup if the Chardonnay is especially dry
- Lemon wheel or twist (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Add gin, Chardonnay, Aperol, and lemon juice to a shaker with ice.
2. Shake briefly, about 8 seconds.
3. Strain into a wine glass filled with fresh ice.
4. Top with sparkling water and stir gently.
