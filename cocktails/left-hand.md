A bourbon Negroni that sits halfway to a Manhattan, with chocolate bitters. The book suggests a spiced chocolate bitters like Fee's Aztec. Created by Sam Ross (Milk & Honey, around 2006).

## Original
- 2 dashes chocolate bitters
- 3/4 oz (22 ml) sweet vermouth
- 3/4 oz (22 ml) Campari
- 1 1/2 oz (45 ml) bourbon
- Garnish: Luxardo cherry

1. Stir all ingredients in a mixing glass with ice until chilled, about 45 seconds.
2. Strain into a chilled coupe or cocktail glass and garnish with a Luxardo cherry.
