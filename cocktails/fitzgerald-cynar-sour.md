A sophisticated bourbon sour with earthy, herbal bitterness and bright citrus. Old Fitzgerald's smooth, wheated bourbon character pairs with Cynar's botanical depth and Aperol's bittersweet orange notes. A touch of maple

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1½ oz Old Fitzgerald bourbon
- ½ oz Cynar
- ½ oz Aperol
- ¾ oz freshly squeezed lemon juice
- ¼ oz maple syrup (optional, for additional sweetness)
- Ice (amount not given)
- Orange or lemon twist, for garnish (optional) (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Add bourbon, Cynar, Aperol, lemon juice, and optional maple syrup to a cocktail shaker.
2. Fill the shaker with ice.
3. Shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds until thoroughly chilled.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe glass.
5. Garnish with an orange or lemon twist, if desired.
