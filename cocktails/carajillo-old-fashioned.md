A split-base rye and cognac old fashioned sweetened with Licor 43 and coffee liqueur, stirred light and poured over a big rock with coffee beans on top. A great nightcap (@TheEducatedBarfly).

## Original
- 1/4 oz (7.5 ml) Licor 43
- 1/4 oz (7.5 ml) coffee liqueur
- 1 oz (30 ml) cognac
- 1 oz (30 ml) rye whiskey
- Garnish: 3 coffee beans

1. Add the Licor 43, coffee liqueur, cognac and rye to a mixing glass.
2. Add ice and stir until chilled. Lean toward under-stirring, since it sits on a large piece of ice and will keep diluting.
3. Pour over a large tempered ice cube in a rocks glass.
4. Garnish with 3 coffee beans on the ice.

## Our version
- [ ] Licor 43 (1/4 oz)
- [ ] coffee liqueur (1/4 oz)
- [ ] cognac (1 oz)
- [ ] rye whiskey (1 oz)
- [ ] coffee beans (3)
- [ ] large clear ice cube

**Steps**
1. Stir the Licor 43 (1/4 oz), coffee liqueur (1/4 oz), cognac (1 oz) and rye (1 oz) with ice in a mixing glass until just chilled. Go a little light, since the big cube adds dilution.
2. Let a large clear cube sit out a minute to temper so it won't crack, then set it in a rocks glass and pour over.
3. Float 3 coffee beans on the ice.
