An adventurous, earthy, bittersweet and orange-forward sour with gin, Cynar, Sapelo Aperitivo, and aquafaba.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1 oz Jam Jam Gin
- ¾ oz Cynar
- ¾ oz Sapelo Aperitivo
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¾ oz aquafaba
- Optional: ¼ oz simple syrup
- Orange or lemon twist, optional (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker without ice.
2. Dry shake vigorously for about 20 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake for another 12–15 seconds.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
5. Let the foam settle briefly before serving.
