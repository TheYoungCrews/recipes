*Also known as the Banana Bread Old Fashioned*

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 2 oz Old Fitzgerald bourbon
- ¼ oz banana liqueur
- 1 barspoon (approximately ⅙ oz) homemade English breakfast tea simple syrup
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- 1 dash orange bitters (optional)
- Orange peel for garnish (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Combine bourbon, banana liqueur, tea syrup, and bitters in a mixing glass.
2. Fill with ice and stir for 20–25 seconds until thoroughly chilled.
3. Strain into a rocks glass over one large ice cube.
4. Express an orange peel over the cocktail and drop it into the glass.
5. Serve immediately. - Bourbon provides a rich, vanilla-and-caramel foundation. - Banana liqueur adds a subtle ripe banana flavor reminiscent of banana bread. - English breakfast tea syrup contributes maltiness and tannins, preventing the drink from becoming overly sweet. - Start with just one barspoon of tea syrup, as the banana liqueur also contributes sweetness. - **Recommended variation:** Substitute 1 dash of black walnut bitters for the orange bitters to create a toasted, nutty banana bread character. - Adjust the banana liqueur depending on its sweetness and intensity.

## Our version
**Grocery & inventory check**
- [ ] 2 oz Rittenhouse rye
- [ ] ¼ oz Giffard banana liqueur
- [ ] 1 barspoon maple syrup
- [ ] 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- [ ] 1 dash black walnut bitters
- [ ] Orange peel for garnish

**Steps**
1. Combine Rittenhouse rye (2 oz), Giffard banana liqueur (¼ oz), maple syrup (1 barspoon), Angostura bitters (2 dashes) and black walnut bitters (1 dash) in a mixing glass.
2. Fill with ice and stir for 20 to 25 seconds until thoroughly chilled.
3. Strain into a rocks glass over one large ice cube.
4. Express an orange peel over the cocktail and drop it into the glass.

### 1L jar batch (about 12 drinks)
Set the jar on a scale and tare between each ingredient:
- 660 g Rittenhouse rye
- 100 g Giffard banana liqueur
- 40 g maple syrup (add 10 g at a time to taste)
- 22 g Angostura bitters (about 24 dashes)
- 11 g black walnut bitters (about 12 dashes)
- pinch of salt

Shake the jar until the maple is fully dissolved.

To serve, stir about 2½ oz with ice for 20 to 25 seconds, strain over one large cube, and express an orange peel over the top. Or add 160 g filtered water to the jar to make it pour-and-serve from the fridge or freezer, about 3 oz over a big cube.

Optional: drop one cinnamon stick in the jar for a day or two, then take it out. Resting overnight helps it meld. If it tastes flat, try a dash of orange bitters in the glass.
