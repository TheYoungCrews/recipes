Rum and rye Old Fashioned with banana liqueur, cacao rum, honey syrup and tiki bitters. Stirred in a highball glass. By Leandro DiMonriva / The Educated Barfly.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 2 dashes Tiki Bitters
- 1/4 oz (7.5 ml) Honey Syrup
- 1/4 oz (7.5 ml) Cacao Rum
- 1/4 oz (7.5 ml) Banana Liqueur
- 3/4 oz (22.5 ml) Overproof Jamaica Rum
- 3/4 oz (22.5 ml) Rye Whiskey
- Garnish: orange twist

**Steps**
1. Add all ingredients to a highball glass.
2. Stir until sufficiently chilled.
3. Garnish with an orange twist.
