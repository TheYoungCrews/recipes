*Also known as the Banana Bread Old Fashioned*

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 2 oz Old Fitzgerald bourbon
- ¼ oz banana liqueur
- 1 barspoon (approximately ⅙ oz) homemade English breakfast tea simple syrup
- 2 dashes Angostura bitters
- 1 dash orange bitters (optional)
- Orange peel for garnish (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Combine bourbon, banana liqueur, tea syrup, and bitters in a mixing glass.
2. Fill with ice and stir for 20–25 seconds until thoroughly chilled.
3. Strain into a rocks glass over one large ice cube.
4. Express an orange peel over the cocktail and drop it into the glass.
5. Serve immediately. - Bourbon provides a rich, vanilla-and-caramel foundation. - Banana liqueur adds a subtle ripe banana flavor reminiscent of banana bread. - English breakfast tea syrup contributes maltiness and tannins, preventing the drink from becoming overly sweet. - Start with just one barspoon of tea syrup, as the banana liqueur also contributes sweetness. - **Recommended variation:** Substitute 1 dash of black walnut bitters for the orange bitters to create a toasted, nutty banana bread character. - Adjust the banana liqueur depending on its sweetness and intensity.
