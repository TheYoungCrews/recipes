An earthy, complex gin sour with Cynar, Aperol, lemon, and a silky aquafaba foam.

## Original

**Ingredients**
- 1½ oz Jam Jam Gin
- ¾ oz Cynar
- ½ oz Aperol
- ¾ oz fresh lemon juice
- ¾ oz aquafaba
- Lemon twist, optional (amount not given)

**Steps**
1. Add all ingredients to a shaker without ice.
2. Dry shake vigorously for 15–20 seconds.
3. Add ice and shake for another 12–15 seconds.
4. Double strain into a chilled coupe.
5. Let the foam settle briefly before serving.
