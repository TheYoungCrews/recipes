# Taste profile (living document)

Agents: read this before writing any "Our version". Update it after feedback, with a date and the recipe that taught us.

## Priority
- **Everyday weeknight dinners first.** Default to 30 minutes or less of active time, one pot / Dutch oven / air fryer, mostly staples, leftovers that hold up. Hosting and party food are secondary.

## Stores
- Costco: bulk proteins, broth, eggs, dairy, oils
- Trader Joe's: shortcuts (frozen, sauces, bread, chili crisp)
- Whole Foods: specialty items, better produce and meat
- Buford Highway Farmers Market: Asian and Latin produce, herbs (Thai basil), sauces, fish sauce, noodles
- Kroger: fallback only
- Briefs should mark non-staple items with a store (`@trader-joes`) and prefer one-stop shopping on weeknights.

## Likes (seed, from the first two briefs)
- Brothy noodle bowls; turmeric-ginger bone broth (Fire Kettle) as a base
- Deep fond and char: blistered tomatoes, hard sears, tomato paste cooked to rust
- Umami boosters: anchovy paste, tomato paste, chili crisp
- Bright finish: lime juice, fresh herbs (Thai basil, green onion)
- Cooling contrast: full-fat cottage cheese
- High-protein swaps: egg white noodles, organ blends
- Crunch: air-fried chicken "katsu" texture, sesame seeds
- Gear: Dutch oven, air fryer
